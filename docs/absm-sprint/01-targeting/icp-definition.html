Ideal Customer Profile — Axians ABSM Program

Context: This ICP defines the target accounts for the Axians–Akamai ABSM program. Axians is the delivery partner; Akamai Guardicore Segmentation is the product. The ICP was designed to identify German Mittelstand manufacturers with the highest probability of purchasing microsegmentation in a 6–12 month window.

Core ICP definition

Industry: Manufacturing — discrete (machines, automotive components, industrial equipment)
Geography: Germany (primary), DACH (secondary)
Revenue: €200M–€5B (upper Mittelstand to lower large enterprise)
Employees: 500–20,000
IT model: On-premises or hybrid (not cloud-native — Guardicore targets workload segmentation on-premises and in cloud)

Qualifying triggers (any 2 = qualify)

Trigger Signal Why it matters
NIS2 scope Manufacturing sector, >250 employees OR critical infrastructure Regulatory mandate creates urgency
TISAX Active automotive supply chain, OEM relationships Information security audit requirement
Prior incident Disclosed cyberattack in last 3 years Risk awareness is activated
OT/IT convergence Active Industry 4.0 / smart factory initiative IT/OT network segmentation is required
IT modernisation SAP S/4HANA migration, cloud lift-and-shift Network topology change creates segmentation project
M&A Recent acquisition or being acquired Network integration = segmentation complexity

Disqualifying signals

  • Cloud-native infrastructure (AWS-first, no on-premises footprint) — Guardicore’s differentiation is on-premises
  • <100 employees — too small for Guardicore licensing economics
  • Already deployed Illumio — compete, don’t displace
  • No Axians touchpoint or warm path — cold outreach only fails at this ticket size

Persona definition

Primary persona: CISO / Head of IT Security

  • Owns the NIS2 compliance programme
  • Responsible for the segmentation architecture decision
  • Measures success by audit pass rate and incident reduction
  • Pain: “I need to demonstrate segmentation to the auditor and to the board”

Secondary persona: CIO / IT Director

  • Controls the IT budget
  • Approves vendor relationships
  • Measures success by project delivery and cost control
  • Pain: “This needs to integrate with SAP and not break production”

Champion persona: Security Architect / IT Infrastructure Lead

  • Evaluates solutions technically
  • Runs the POC
  • Influences the vendor decision
  • Pain: “I need something that works on our legacy infrastructure without agents”
