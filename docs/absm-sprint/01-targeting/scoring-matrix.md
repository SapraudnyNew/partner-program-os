# Target Account Scoring Matrix

**Purpose:** Score prospective accounts against the ICP to determine inclusion in the program.  
**Scale:** Each criterion scored 0–3. Maximum score = 30. Threshold for inclusion: ≥ 18.

---

## Scoring criteria

| # | Criterion | Weight | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | NIS2 scope | 3× | Not in scope | Probable scope | Confirmed scope | Confirmed + board-level awareness |
| C2 | TISAX requirement | 2× | Not applicable | Indirect (supply chain) | Direct (OEM-mandated) | Active audit underway |
| C3 | OT/IT footprint | 2× | IT-only | Separate OT (no convergence) | Converging OT/IT | Fully converged / smart factory |
| C4 | Prior incident / risk event | 2× | None known | Industry incident awareness | Own disclosed incident | Recent high-profile incident |
| C5 | Axians relationship | 3× | No relationship | Prospect in CRM | Active project history | Ongoing managed service |
| C6 | IT modernisation signal | 1× | Legacy static | Single workstream | Active SAP/cloud migration | Multi-stream transformation |
| C7 | Revenue / size | 1× | <€200M | €200–500M | €500M–2B | >€2B |
| C8 | Headcount IT team | 1× | <10 | 10–50 | 50–200 | >200 |
| C9 | Decision speed | 2× | >24 months typical | 12–24 months | 6–12 months | <6 months (trigger event present) |
| C10 | Competitor presence | 2× | Illumio active | PANW segmentation | Other/none | No microseg vendor at all |

**Maximum raw score:** 19 (sum of weights × 3)  
**Normalised to 30** for reporting
