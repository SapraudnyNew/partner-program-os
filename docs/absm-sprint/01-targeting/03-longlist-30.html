03 · 30-Company Longlist

Stage 1 deliverable · D3-2 ABSM Sprint
Purpose: Real, named German Mittelstand manufacturers researched via web. The set the scoring matrix will be applied to.
Date of figures: Most recent publicly available, typically FY2023–FY2024. Some figures are estimates marked with confidence indicators.

Method

I researched verified firmographics from primary and secondary sources: Wikipedia, company websites, ZoomInfo, North Data, Owler, industry publications, and Axians’ own customer reference page. Every company below is real and operating; revenue and headcount are sourced to public references. Confidence markers indicate data quality:

  • ✅ Confirmed via multiple sources, recent
  • ⚠️ Single-source, recent
  • 🧠 Estimate / inferred from related data points

Hard filters applied at this stage (will exclude in scoring matrix if violated):
- Headquartered in Germany ✓
- Revenue €100M–€2B (some borderline cases included for scoring discrimination)
- Employees 1,000–10,000
- NIS2-relevant manufacturing sector
- Excluded: DAX 40 constituents, large MDAX, KRITIS-only operators, pure software/services

Notes on the longlist composition

Geographic spread:
- Baden-Württemberg (BW): 14 companies — the historical Mittelstand heartland, close to Axians Stuttgart, Mannheim, Ulm offices
- Bavaria (BY): 5 companies — concentrated in mechanical engineering and packaging
- North Rhine-Westphalia (NRW): 8 companies — automotive and electrical
- Niedersachsen (NI): 3 companies — automation, drives

Sub-sector spread:
- Mechanical engineering & machine tools: 10
- Industrial components & drives: 5
- Electrical equipment & connectivity: 2
- Automotive suppliers: 3
- Industrial process / specialty machinery: 5
- Vehicle (non-passenger) manufacturing: 2
- Other: 3

Why a few borderline-band companies are included (e.g. Phoenix Contact, Schmitz Cargobull, Kostal):
These are intentionally retained so the scoring matrix can demonstrate it correctly down-ranks above-band companies via Criterion 3 (Revenue Band Fit). They serve as a control set; matrix-eligible accounts should clearly outscore them.
