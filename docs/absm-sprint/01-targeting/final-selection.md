# Final Account Selection — Rationale

**Selected accounts:** Hörmann · Maschinenfabrik Reinhausen · Witte Automotive · TRUMPF

---

## Selection rationale

### Why these four

All four accounts meet the following compound criteria:
1. **NIS2 confirmed scope** — all are manufacturers with >250 employees in Germany
2. **OT/IT convergence** — all have active Industry 4.0 or smart factory programmes
3. **No Illumio presence identified** — clean competitive field for Guardicore
4. **Axians touchpoint exists or is buildable** — either existing relationship or clear warm path
5. **Decision-making unit is identifiable** — CISO/CIO contacts are findable from public sources

### Why not others from the shortlist

The shortlist of 10 was filtered based on:
- **Companies dropped for Illumio presence:** 2 accounts had confirmed Illumio POC or deployment
- **Companies dropped for cloud-native architecture:** 1 account was AWS-first, incompatible with Guardicore on-premises positioning
- **Companies dropped for no Axians path:** 3 accounts had no warm entry point for Axians; cold outreach at this ticket size fails
