MDF Allocation Specification — Axians × Akamai Guardicore PIP

Stage 5 Infrastructure · D3-2 ABSM Sprint
Program: Partner Intelligence Program — DACH Sprint
Fund type: Akamai MDF (Marketing Development Fund) via partner portal
Budget horizon: 12 months from program activation

Purpose of This Document

Akamai’s partner program allocates Marketing Development Funds to partners who co-invest in demand generation activities. This document specifies how Axians should structure, request, and deploy MDF for the Guardicore PIP — ensuring every euro of spend is recoverable, measurable, and tied to pipeline creation.

Key principle: MDF is a cost-sharing mechanism, not a budget substitute. Activities below are structured for 50/50 Akamai/Axians co-investment, with Axians anchoring spend to activities Akamai is already incentivized to fund.

Activity Specifications

Category 1 — Account-Based Events (€24,000 total / €12,000 MDF)

1.1 Mittelstand Segmentation Roundtable — NRW

Budget: €8,000 (€4,000 MDF)
Format: Invitation-only dinner event · 12–16 CISO/IT Security leads
Location: Düsseldorf or Cologne — neutral venue
Timing: Q2 · May/June
Attendees: Target 2 contacts from active PIP accounts (Hörmann, Witte) + 8–10 net-new prospects from same ICP
Agenda structure:
- 30 min networking pre-dinner
- 20 min NIS2 deadline briefing (Axians IT Security practice lead)
- 20 min Guardicore OT segmentation demo (Akamai overlay)
- 60 min dinner + facilitated discussion
- 10 min Victorinox case study video (Stefan Epp reference)

Akamai MDF justification: Joint demand generation event; Akamai overlay presenter included in cost basis; leads entered into both Axians CRM and Akamai partner portal.

Expected output: 3–5 qualified follow-up conversations; 1 Hörmann or Witte meeting confirmed.

1.2 TISAX / Automotive Segmentation Session — NRW Auto Cluster

Budget: €6,000 (€3,000 MDF)
Format: Breakfast briefing · 8–12 Tier 1/Tier 2 OEM supplier IT leads
Location: Velbert or Wuppertal (automotive corridor)
Timing: Q2 · May
Anchor content: ISO/SAE 21434 + NIS2 combined obligation brief (PDF-03 + PDF-01)
Primary target: Witte Automotive — invitation via Patrick Demant or Rainer Schulten
Secondary targets: Hella, Brose, ZF Friedrichshafen contacts in region

Expected output: Witte Automotive discovery meeting; 2 new NRW automotive accounts in HubSpot.

1.3 Executive Roundtable — BW Manufacturing Leaders

Budget: €10,000 (€5,000 MDF)
Format: Half-day workshop + networking lunch · 8–10 CIOs / CISOs

MDF Request & Claims Process

Submission Requirements (Akamai Partner Portal)

For each activity, submit the following to Akamai DACH channel manager before execution:

Document Required
Activity brief (this spec, per section) Yes
Pre-approval form via Akamai partner portal Yes
Estimated ROI / pipeline impact Yes
Post-activity report (within 30 days) Yes — required for reimbursement

Claims Documentation per Activity

  • Events: invitation list, registration count, post-event survey results, photo evidence
  • Content: asset PDF, distribution metrics (downloads, form fills, LinkedIn engagement)
  • Digital: Google Ads / LinkedIn screenshots, CRM lead count, UTM conversion report
  • Trade shows: booth photos, meeting log (company, attendee, outcome), dinner receipt

Reimbursement Timeline

Akamai DACH processes MDF claims within 45 days of activity completion and documentation submission. Axians finance to plan cash flow accordingly — front-fund activities; claim after post-activity report approval.
