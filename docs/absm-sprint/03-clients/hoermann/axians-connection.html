Hörmann KG — Axians Connection

Account 1 · Stage 2 Intel · D3-2 ABSM Sprint
Purpose: Map the existing Axians–Hörmann relationship, identify the cross-portfolio gap, and define the path from current state to a Guardicore conversation.

TL;DR

Hörmann KG VKG has been an Axians customer since 2014 — over 11 years. The relationship is delivered through Axians NEO Solutions & Technology GmbH, focused entirely on SAP service management consulting and customer cloud platforms. There is no current Axians IT Security engagement at Hörmann. The Guardicore opportunity therefore requires internal Axians cross-portfolio orchestration: routing from Axians NEO → Axians IT Security Services (Alain de Pauw’s organization). This is exactly the Partner Intelligence Program (PIP) workflow this sprint is designed to demonstrate.

The cross-portfolio referral problem

For an Axians NEO account manager working with Hörmann’s Alexandra Kempe to introduce Alain de Pauw’s IT Security organization, the following needs to happen internally:

  1. Trigger: Account manager recognizes a NIS2/security opportunity at Hörmann
  2. Routing: Account manager has a known internal contact in Axians IT Security
  3. Joint planning: Both portfolios align on offer, pricing, and engagement model
  4. Customer intro: Joint meeting positioned to customer as “Axians broadening engagement”
  5. Compensation: Internal credit-split mechanism prevents one portfolio “stealing” the relationship

The friction: Internal cross-portfolio handoff in large multi-portfolio integrators is consistently the weakest link. Account managers are compensated on their own portfolio’s revenue; introducing a sister portfolio that may “take customer time” away from their own work is structurally disincentivized unless management actively rewards it.

The PIP architecture solves exactly this problem

Recall from Stage 0: the Partner Intelligence Program (PIP) is a two-layer Channel ABM initiative:

  • Layer 1 — Partner Intent Routing: Akamai shares high-intent DACH accounts with Axians, segmented by topic
  • Layer 2 — Account-Based Enablement: Pre-built sales kits per account, including the cross-portfolio narrative

For Hörmann specifically, PIP Layer 1 would surface Hörmann as “in-market for OT segmentation” and route to both Axians NEO (existing relationship) and Axians IT Security (target portfolio). PIP Layer 2 would provide pre-built materials specifically designed for the cross-portfolio handoff: a one-page brief for Kempe/Eichler explaining why their colleague Redinger should meet de Pauw’s team.

This is the PIP use case par excellence: Akamai’s intent + content delivery enables Axians to systematically do the cross-portfolio orchestration that today happens only sporadically through individual account-manager initiative.

What an Approach Plan Looks Like

Phase 1 — Internal Axians alignment (Week 1–2)

Materials required:
- One-page “Why Hörmann” brief flagging the triple obligation (NIS2 + KRITIS cascade + TISAX) and the existing 11-year Axians-NEO trust
- Joint executive talking points for Axians NEO leadership × Axians IT Security leadership
- Pre-agreed credit-split commercial framework

Owner: Internal Axians MDF/program manager — likely under the Cyber Security division leader (Alain de Pauw)

Phase 2 — Joint Axians team formation (Week 2–4)

Required:
- Axians NEO account manager + Axians IT Security senior + Akamai overlay
- Joint pre-call: identify the right meeting type (workshop? executive briefing? POC scoping?)
- Coordinated outreach plan via Kempe (NEO side) and direct LinkedIn (IT Security side)

Phase 3 — Customer-facing introduction (Week 4–6)

The recommended ask of Hörmann: a 60-minute joint session featuring:
- Axians NEO account team (familiar, low-risk for customer)
- Axians IT Security colleague (new portfolio, brought in by trusted Axians)
- Akamai overlay (Guardicore product depth)
- Outcome: agreement to scope a NIS2 readiness assessment OR an OT segmentation workshop OR a Guardicore demo — depending on Redinger’s stated current priority

Phase 4 — POC scoping (Week 6–12)

Based on the customer’s response:
- If POC interest: scope a 30-day proof of value in a single Hörmann production site or single Hörmann Cloud subscription
- If assessment interest: scope a NIS2 segmentation gap assessment as a paid Axians services engagement (Guardicore as recommended tool)
- If workshop interest: half-day cyber workshop, leads to either of the above
