## Strategic context

Hörmann is Europe's largest manufacturer of building closure systems. The company has expanded significantly into smart building technology — their products increasingly include IoT controllers, remote management platforms, and building management system (BMS) integrations. This OT/IT convergence is the core security risk: production PLCs for manufacturing and IoT door controllers are now reachable from the corporate network.

As a private family company with 30+ production sites, Hörmann manages a distributed OT landscape with varying levels of IT security maturity across locations.
