Maschinenfabrik Reinhausen — Relationship Map

Account 2 · Stage 2 Intel · D3-2 ABSM Sprint
Purpose: LinkedIn-verified decision makers. Reinhausen is a cold account — no warm Axians path — so the relationship map matters more than for Hörmann; this is the people picture from scratch.

Strategic Framing

For Reinhausen, the buying motion is specification-led, technical-evaluator-driven. Dr. Hubert Feyrer’s team is the audience, not the executive board. The board will sign off, but the technical merits decide whether the deal happens.

Three concentric circles matter:
1. Inner circle — technical evaluators who validate Guardicore’s fit (Feyrer, Schneider, Mascha, Ach)
2. Middle circle — sponsors who authorize budget and time (Maier-Scheubeck, Breuer, Michalka)
3. Outer circle — adjacent stakeholders who can either block or accelerate (Hengl for SAP, IT director equivalent if identifiable)

What’s Notable About This Map

Three observations:

  1. No identified CISO at corporate level. Dr. Feyrer holds the public cyber title but in product/automation context. Either MR has a corporate CISO who isn’t public-facing, or the function is distributed. Stage 4 discovery should confirm — and may reveal a key contact not yet visible.

  2. The technical leadership has 10–20-year MR tenures. Feyrer’s articles, Schneider’s 16 years, Hengl’s tenure on S/4HANA, Gebauer’s 17 years. Translation: trust is built slowly here. Vendor evaluation will be thorough. Pre-sales engineering quality matters more than commercial creativity.

  3. The family ownership is real but professionalized. The Scheubeck Holding owns 100%, but the family doesn’t appear in the executive team. Decisions go through Maier-Scheubeck and the board, not the family directly. The buying motion is corporate, not family-business intimate.
