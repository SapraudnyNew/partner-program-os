Maschinenfabrik Reinhausen — Axians Connection (Cold Account Assessment)

Account 2 · Stage 2 Intel · D3-2 ABSM Sprint
Purpose: Honestly assess the absence of an Axians–MR relationship and define the cold-outreach approach.

TL;DR

No public Axians–Reinhausen relationship exists. Reinhausen’s verified IT integrator stack includes All for One (SAP), SNP (migration), PIKON (SAP consulting), Solvares (field service), and Siemens (Xcelerator partnership for TESSA). Axians is conspicuously absent. This makes MR a cold-but-bullseye account: a perfect ICP fit with no warm-path bridge. The approach must be technical-credibility-first, not relationship-leveraged. This is exactly the kind of account where the Partner Intelligence Program’s intent-signal layer would create the first connection.

What’s in Reinhausen’s Integrator Slot Today

The competitive landscape MR has already chosen:

Integrator Service Strength relative to Axians
All for One Group SAP S/4HANA implementation + ongoing Strong Mittelstand SAP focus; deep S/4 expertise; comparable to Axians SAP Solutions but more SAP-pure
SNP Migration platform (Kyano, Crystal Bridge), Bluefield approach Specialized — not a competitor to Axians security

Siemens (Xcelerator) Industrial software platform for TESSA APM Strategic, hard to displace; Siemens is product-co-development partner not just integrator

The competitive read: All for One is the main IT services player at Reinhausen. They are a strong, well-respected Mittelstand-focused IT integrator. However:

  • All for One’s portfolio is SAP-centric. Their cyber security offering exists but is narrower than Axians IT Security Services.
  • All for One doesn’t have a comparable ISG Benchmark Leader position in cyber.
  • All for One doesn’t have NVIDIA BlueField OT integration in their announced portfolio.

Translation: Axians enters the Reinhausen conversation not by displacing All for One but by adding a complementary capability — network-level cyber security and Guardicore microsegmentation — that All for One does not natively offer.

Why Reinhausen Is Still the Right Account Despite the Cold-Start

Five reasons this account makes sense even without a warm path:

1. Technical-credibility-led culture (✅ verified)

Reinhausen’s public posture is heavily standards-led (IEC 62443, BDEW, ISO 27001, IEEE 1686). Dr. Feyrer publishes technical articles. The S/4HANA migration was decided on technical merits. This is a culture where a strong technical case wins, not a relationship-led culture.

Axians IT Security has the standards positioning to compete on Feyrer’s terms:
- ISG Leader 2025 in 4 cyber categories
- ISO 27001-certified SOCs (Hamburg, Ulm)
- Public Guardicore partnership with case studies (Hochland, IAV, fischerwerke)
- Established IEC 62443 OT segmentation experience

2. The buying triggers are converging (✅ verified)

Three live triggers at MR:
- S/4HANA migration completed January 2026
- Investment offensive doubling Regensburg by Q4 2025+
- ISO 27001 ISMS being built for ETOS

Any one of these would justify a segmentation conversation. All three together create maximum urgency.

3. No incumbent locked in (✅ verified — no opposing signal)

No public reference to Illumio, Akamai-direct, or any microsegmentation vendor at MR. This is rare for a company at this scale of digital sophistication and suggests the white space is real, not occupied.

4. PIP architecture is designed for exactly this (✅ strategic)

The Partner Intelligence Program’s purpose is to surface accounts where:
- Akamai detects intent signals (search behavior, content engagement, recent regulatory events)
- The account lacks an existing Axians relationship
- The fit is high

Reinhausen is the canonical example. Stage 4 outreach demonstrates exactly how the PIP routing layer would inject a “Hot 20” account into Axians’ pipeline.

5. Portfolio storytelling value (✅ strategic)

Pairing Hörmann (existing customer cross-sell) with Reinhausen (cold ICP bullseye) demonstrates the full PIP playbook — both motions, not just one. The sprint becomes more credible as a partner-marketing case for that reason.

How Axians Could Approach (3 sequenced motions)

  1. Axians IT Security publishes (or amplifies) a public position paper on IEC 62443 zone/conduit segmentation for the energy supply chain — directly relevant to MR’s audience
  2. Dr. Feyrer is invited to comment / contribute via direct LinkedIn message: “Reading your ETOS ISMS articles — could we co-publish a perspective on supply-chain segmentation for Annex I manufacturers?“
  3. Conversation moves from publication invitation → technical workshop → POC scoping
  4. The intellectual-peer framing aligns with Feyrer’s published style

Timeline: 8–12 weeks from publication to first joint meeting

Motion 2 — Event-led introduction (parallel)

  1. Confirm MR representation at OT Security Day München (July 21, 2026)
  2. Pre-position with a personalized invitation
  3. Reserve a 1:1 meeting slot for Dr. Feyrer or Stefan Schneider at the event
  4. Use the event meeting to scope a follow-up technical workshop

Timeline: Synchronized with event dates

Motion 3 — Investment-narrative outreach to operations (executive layer)

  1. Holger Michalka (MD Operations) receives an executive briefing offer focused on “Protecting the investment offensive — segmentation architecture for the doubling-of-capacity”
  2. Lower probability of response than technical paths but higher executive sponsorship if successful

Recommended sequence: Motion 1 as the primary investment; Motion 2 as a parallel track that compresses the timeline if event timing aligns; Motion 3 only after technical credibility is established.

What an “Account Activation” Would Look Like

If Stage 4 outreach succeeds at opening the conversation, the activation sequence would be:

Phase 1 — First technical conversation (week 0)

60-minute call with Dr. Feyrer + 1-2 of his team
- Axians-side: Axians IT Security senior architect + Akamai overlay
- Topic: IEC 62443 alignment, Guardicore architecture overview, MR’s specific S/4HANA segmentation needs
- Outcome: Agreement to a technical workshop

Phase 2 — Joint architecture workshop (week 3–4)

Half-day on-site or virtual
- Axians-side: Plus Stefan Schneider (Head of System Architecture) and likely Christian Hengl (SAP)
- Walk through proposed segmentation architecture for SAP S/4HANA + manufacturing OT + TESSA cloud boundary
- Outcome: POC scope agreement

Phase 3 — Proof of value (week 5–12)

30–60-day Guardicore POC in a single environment (likely SAP S/4HANA in non-production, or a single production cell)
- Axians-side: Axians IT Security engineer + Akamai SE on-call
- Outcome: Demonstrated segmentation visibility, policy enforcement, no production impact

Phase 4 — Commercial scoping (week 12+)

  • ROI analysis using Forrester TEI as anchor
  • Forrester $1B-revenue / 5,000-employee composite is directly comparable to MR’s profile
  • Three-year financial case: ~152% ROI, $9.6M total benefit, 6-month payback
  • Translation: For MR specifically, a credible argument that Year 1 ACV of €500K–€1M generates €3-4M of operational risk reduction + NIS2 audit confidence
