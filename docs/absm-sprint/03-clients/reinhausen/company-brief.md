## Strategic context

Reinhausen is the world market leader in on-load tap changers for power transformers — a hidden champion in the truest sense. They supply the critical components that regulate voltage in electrical grids globally. This positions them as both a **critical infrastructure supplier** and a **manufacturing company with OT exposure**.

Key security risk: Reinhausen's products manage power grid voltage. Intellectual property (product design, manufacturing processes) is extremely high-value. A successful cyberattack on their R&D or manufacturing systems would have direct critical infrastructure implications.
