## Pain inventory

| Pain | Category | Urgency | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dual NIS2 scope: manufacturer + critical infra supplier | Regulatory | Very High | Confirmed NIS2 scope (both categories) |
| IP protection: world-leading tap changer designs | Strategic | Very High | Market leader position, high IP value |
| Critical infrastructure implications of any breach | Reputational | High | Products embedded in national grids |
| OT security: precision manufacturing with network connectivity | Technical | High | Complex manufacturing environment |
| TISAX potential | Regulatory | Medium | Automotive supply chain ⚠️ |

## Primary pain

**"We supply critical infrastructure globally. A breach of our manufacturing systems or IP would have implications beyond our company. How do we ensure our internal networks are properly segmented to contain that risk?"**

This is a board-level conversation, not just a CISO conversation. The critical infrastructure dimension elevates security from IT issue to business continuity risk.

## Guardicore conversation approach

Position Guardicore as **IP protection + NIS2 compliance** in one deployment:
- Segment R&D network from production network from corporate network
- Provide visibility into what's talking to what (insider threat + APT detection)
- NIS2 Article 21 documentation built in
