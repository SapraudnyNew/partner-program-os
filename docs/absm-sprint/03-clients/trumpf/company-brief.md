## Strategic context

TRUMPF is Germany's largest privately held machine tool manufacturer and the global market leader in laser cutting systems. The company's "TRUMPF Smart Factory" initiative is a reference implementation for Industry 4.0 — they digitise their own production and sell the expertise to customers. This creates maximum OT/IT convergence risk.

**Prior incident:** TRUMPF was hit by the Emotet/Ryuk ransomware campaign in early 2019. The attack forced a partial shutdown of operations and was publicly disclosed. This is the defining security context for any Guardicore conversation.
