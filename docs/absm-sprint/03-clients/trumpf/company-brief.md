## What Trumpf Makes

### 1. Machine Tools (€2.4B FY24/25 — 56% of revenue)
- 2D/3D laser cutting machines (world leader)
- Punching, bending, combined machines
- Sheet-metal-processing systems and automation
- **TruConnect** networked production platform
- **TruTops Fab** manufacturing execution system (MES)

### 2. Laser Technology (€1.2B FY24/25 — 28% of revenue)
- Industrial high-power lasers (CO₂, fiber, disk lasers)
- Specialty lasers for medical, scientific applications

### 3. EUV Light Sources (€724M FY24/25 — 17% of revenue) ⚡
- **Trumpf is the sole supplier of EUV light sources to ASML**
- ASML's EUV scanners are the only machines that can manufacture <7nm chips (TSMC, Samsung, Intel all depend on this)
- Trumpf sits at the apex of the global semiconductor supply chain
- Revenue down 23% in FY24/25 due to ASML inventory cycle; structural significance unchanged

### 4. Electronics (€442M FY24/25)
- Power electronics; carved out as separate business field
