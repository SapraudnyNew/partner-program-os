Trumpf SE + Co. KG — Axians Connection

Trumpf SE + Co. KG — Axians Connection

Showcase Account · Stage 2 Intel · D3-2 ABSM Sprint
Purpose: Confirm the (absent) Axians–Trumpf relationship and explain why Trumpf illustrates the direct Akamai-led motion — the partner-less variant of the playbook.

TL;DR

No Axians–Trumpf relationship. Trumpf is above the partner-led ICP band and operates with a mature internal procurement function. At this scale, vendor relationships are managed directly through enterprise accounts. The sprint includes Trumpf as the showcase precisely because it represents a different motion — the Akamai enterprise-direct path that exists in parallel with the Axians partner path. The two motions cover different bands of the Mittelstand market.

The insight: The Axians partner program needs to cover the middle band (€100M–€2B) where enterprise direct is too expensive and indirect is too thin. Hörmann, Reinhausen, and Witte are examples in that band. Trumpf is the showcase just above it — what the methodology would look like at maximum scale.

What the Showcase Demonstrates About the PIP Program

If the Partner Intelligence Program (PIP) is the mechanism that surfaces Mittelstand accounts to Axians, then Trumpf illustrates the ceiling — the accounts that route to Akamai enterprise rather than the partner. The program architecture needs a routing logic:

Account profile Route
<€100M revenue Axians transactional
€100M–€2B, family-owned, regional, DACH-HQ Axians partner-led (PIP target)
>€2B, global, professional management Akamai enterprise-direct (or co-sell)
Above partner band but in strategic named accounts Akamai enterprise direct + partner support

For Trumpf at €4.3B: Akamai enterprise accounts team in DACH + co-sell Axians IT Security if Axians has relationship touchpoints. But the originating motion is Akamai-direct.

What Trumpf’s Posture Teaches the Partner Program

Four lessons for the PIP from the Trumpf analysis:

1. Technical thought leadership is the entry at every maturity level

Trumpf’s own Cyber Security PDF is thought leadership. Their customer engagement is thought leadership. The same pattern works for Axians: publishing technical guidance (IEC 62443 segmentation, NIS2 implementation guides) attracts attention from Feyrer (Reinhausen), Schwarz (Witte), and Redinger (Hörmann).

2. The “teach your customers what you do internally” virtuous cycle

Trumpf publishes OT security guidance to its machine-tool customers. Axians can do the same: publish Guardicore implementation guides for German Mittelstand manufacturers, then surface those guides to their own customer base via PIP.

3. 80 information security coordinators = what a mature deployment looks like

Trumpf’s 80+ coordinator network is the organizational model for a Guardicore deployment at scale. Stage 4 execution materials for Reinhausen, Witte, Hörmann can use Trumpf’s org as the “future state” aspiration — “this is what a segmentation-mature Mittelstand IT organization looks like.”

4. CRA is the next NIS2 — and Trumpf is already preparing

Trumpf’s public CRA discussion (via Caleta’s LinkedIn) signals where the regulatory conversation is heading. PIP should anticipate CRA-triggered demand by Q4 2026 and position Axians IT Security as the CRA-readiness partner now, before the demand wave.

Why This Is the Right Showcase Design

Five properties that make the Trumpf showcase choice defensible:

  1. Named and verifiable — every claim backed by a public source
  2. Above the partner band — shows the methodology extends beyond the ICP
  3. Maximum Guardicore relevance — EUV + Smart Factory + ISO 27001 + NIS2 all converge
  4. Current cyber leadership verified — Caleta (CISO), Guthier (Manager Information Security), Muenster (former Deputy CISO) all LinkedIn-confirmed
  5. Demonstrates market segmentation — Trumpf = the ceiling that justifies the two-band program architecture
