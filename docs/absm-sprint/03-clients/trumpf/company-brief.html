What Trumpf Makes

1. Machine Tools (€2.4B FY24/25 — 56% of revenue)

  • 2D/3D laser cutting machines (world leader)
  • Punching, bending, combined machines
  • Sheet-metal-processing systems and automation
  • TruConnect networked production platform
  • TruTops Fab manufacturing execution system (MES)

2. Laser Technology (€1.2B FY24/25 — 28% of revenue)

  • Industrial high-power lasers (CO₂, fiber, disk lasers)
  • Specialty lasers for medical, scientific applications

3. EUV Light Sources (€724M FY24/25 — 17% of revenue) ⚡

  • Trumpf is the sole supplier of EUV light sources to ASML
  • ASML’s EUV scanners are the only machines that can manufacture <7nm chips (TSMC, Samsung, Intel all depend on this)
  • Trumpf sits at the apex of the global semiconductor supply chain
  • Revenue down 23% in FY24/25 due to ASML inventory cycle; structural significance unchanged

4. Electronics (€442M FY24/25)

  • Power electronics; carved out as separate business field

Why Trumpf Is the Right Showcase

Five reasons for including Trumpf as the named showcase:

1. Recognizable flagship name

One of the few German Mittelstand-spirit manufacturers globally recognized. A portfolio piece that names Trumpf signals depth and confidence.

2. Public information richness

Trumpf publishes annual reports, regular press, leadership profiles, and a public Cyber Security PDF. Every claim in this brief is verifiable to a primary source. Methodology demonstration without privacy compromise.

3. Demonstrates the methodology at maximum maturity

The methodology must produce useful intel even when the account has already done the work. Trumpf is the “already mature” data point.

4. EUV + NVIDIA BlueField angle

Trumpf’s EUV business involves un-agentable specialty industrial equipment at the cutting edge of physics. The Akamai + NVIDIA BlueField agentless OT solution (GA Q2 2026) has a unique angle here — securing the most exotic OT in the world.

5. Cross-references throughout the sprint

  • Trumpf is in Baden-Württemberg → geographic complement to NRW + BY accounts
  • Trumpf is the inspiration for German Industrie 4.0 narrative that Reinhausen won the award for
  • Trumpf is publishing OT security guidance to its customers — a mirror of what Axians + Akamai aspire to do for Trumpf’s Mittelstand customer base

Why Trumpf Is NOT a Live Outreach Target

Honest treatment:

Above the partner-led band

€4.3B / 18,303 employees = named-account direct territory. Akamai enterprise sales (not partner) handles this directly.

Strategic significance creates approval friction

Any Guardicore engagement would face multiple gates: family governance, board sign-off, export control review (given EUV position), and customer-impact assessment. Multi-quarter motion, not Mittelstand 60-day cycle.

Mature cyber org may have already evaluated competitors

80+ information security coordinators + a public CISO + recent ISO 27001 = a buying committee likely already aware of Guardicore and Illumio.

The methodology shines without claiming the deal

The portfolio value comes from demonstrating thorough research on a public flagship, not from claiming Trumpf as a target. Anyone reading the sprint can verify every claim. That credibility is the asset.
