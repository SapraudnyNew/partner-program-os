## Primary pain

**Post-incident, TRUMPF has been investing in security. The question is: have they addressed lateral movement specifically, or did they invest in perimeter and endpoint (the typical post-incident pattern)?**

The 2019 Ryuk attack spread laterally inside the network. The post-incident investment pattern typically covers: better backup, EDR, email security. Lateral movement prevention (microsegmentation) is often missed because it requires a network architecture conversation, not a quick product purchase.

**The ask:** "Since 2019, you've hardened the perimeter and the endpoint. Has the lateral movement layer been addressed? Guardicore's specific capability is preventing exactly what Ryuk did — moving from initial compromise to production systems."

## Risk note

TRUMPF likely has existing security vendor relationships and potentially internal security maturity above the Mittelstand average. This is a more sophisticated conversation. The Axians account must know their security landscape before the first meeting.
