## Strategic context

Witte is a Tier 1 supplier to major European OEMs. Their OEM relationships create a hard TISAX requirement — any Tier 1 supplier to VW Group must hold TISAX certification at the required level. This makes TISAX the primary urgency driver, ahead of NIS2.

Mid-size for a Tier 1 supplier: large enough to be TISAX-mandatory, not large enough to have a mature in-house security function. This creates a SI-dependency — they need Axians to implement and potentially manage the compliance programme.

## NIS2 + Regulatory status

- **NIS2 scope:** Confirmed — manufacturing sector, >250 employees ✅
- **TISAX:** Mandatory — VW Group / Stellantis supplier ⚠️ (confirm level)
- **Dual deadline:** NIS2 + TISAX audit cycle may coincide — strong urgency
