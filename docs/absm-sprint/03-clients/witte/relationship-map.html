Witte Automotive — Relationship Map

Account 3 · Stage 2 Intel · D3-2 ABSM Sprint
Purpose: LinkedIn-verified contacts and decision-maker mapping. Witte’s cyber org appears distributed across multiple senior roles, not centralized in a single CISO.

Strategic Framing

For Witte, the cybersecurity buying motion is compliance-led and customer-driven. The framing that resonates is “proof of controls across five regulatory frameworks”, not “risk avoidance”. The CSMS organization is mature; vendor evaluations will be process-driven and thorough.

Three concentric circles:
1. Inner circle — cyber and IT leaders (Schwarz, Heisig, Demant) who evaluate the technical and compliance fit
2. Middle circle — sponsors (Gölz, Sprute, Janik) who authorize budget
3. Outer circle — adjacent stakeholders (Wagner for quality/compliance, Karas for technology)
