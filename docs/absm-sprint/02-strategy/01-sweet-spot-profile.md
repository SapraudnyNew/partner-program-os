## The Platonic Sweet-Spot Account

A company that would score at the top of the matrix and close the fastest:

**Firmographics:**
- Revenue: **€300M–€1.2B** (sweet band, not at extremes)
- Employees: **2,000–6,000** worldwide
- HQ: **Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria, or North Rhine-Westphalia**
- Ownership: **Family-owned or Stiftung** — private decision-making, long-term orientation
- Founded: **>40 years** — not a startup; established OT infrastructure

**Regulatory profile:**
- Clearly an **"important entity"** under BSIG (NIS2 transposition) — manufacturing sector, revenue cleared
- Either a **TISAX-obligated** automotive supplier OR a supplier to KRITIS operators (utilities, emergency services)
- Registration deadline passed (March 6, 2026) — should have registered, compliance clock is ticking
- **Has NOT yet published a public NIS2 implementation statement** — still building their response

**OT/IT convergence:**
- **Multiple production plants** (3+) each with their own OT network
- Evidence of **Industrie 4.0 initiative** — connected machines, cloud telemetry, smart factory language in press
- Uses **KUKA robotics** or equivalent industrial automation with known OT network presence
- **IoT product line** or IoT-connected products that create an external attack surface (doors, locks, valves, sensors, automation systems)

**Security posture:**
- Has a **named security function** — either a CISO, a Leiter IT Security, or a dedicated MR-CERT equivalent
- That function was **created within the last 3 years** — still building
- Has **SoSafe, KnowBe4, or similar awareness training** — shows investment but awareness ≠ architecture
- May have **ISO 27001 scoped to specific product or business unit** — not yet group-wide
- **No known microsegmentation vendor** — first-mover advantage available

**Axians relationship:**
- **Existing Axians customer in ANY portfolio line** — SAP, network, cloud, digital workspace
- OR: Spoke at the same industry event as Axians (Hannover Messe, it-sa, VDW, ZVEI)
- OR: In a BW or BY cluster where Axians has concentrated offices

## The Three Trigger Events That Surface Sweet-Spot Accounts

In a live PIP deployment, these are the events that would generate a new account alert:

### Trigger 1 — Job posting: "Leiter IT Security" or "CISO" (first-time role)
**What it signals:** The security function is being formalized — likely in response to NIS2 awareness, TISAX audit, or cyber insurance renewal. The company is actively building a security roadmap. They are evaluating tools.
**Lead time to first conversation:** 4–8 weeks (before the new hire has committed to a direction)
**Example:** Rainer Schulten at Witte created exactly this signal in January 2024

### Trigger 2 — Announced Industrie 4.0 / Smart Factory initiative
**What it signals:** Production floor connectivity is being built or expanded. OT/IT convergence is now real, not theoretical. The CISO/IT Security lead will shortly discover their flat-network exposure.
**Lead time to first conversation:** 6–12 weeks after announcement
**Example:** MR's Industrie 4.0 award signals this was done; the window was 2–5 years ago — now the NIS2 review creates a secondary opening

### Trigger 3 — Peer-sector ransomware incident (public disclosure in same sub-sector)
**What it signals:** Boards and CISOs are immediately asked "could this happen to us?" The company is in active evaluation mode.
**Lead time to first conversation:** 2–4 weeks (very short window; act fast)
**Example:** Not observed in current accounts but typical in automotive supplier, food processing, and mechanical engineering clusters

- The ransomware cost is concrete and relatable (not abstract "risk reduction")
- Asking for the methodology, not the conclusion, respects the person's intelligence

## The One Visual That Closes the POC Ask

At the end of the first meeting, the ask for a POC is most successful when framed with a visual like this:

```
Discovery Phase (Week 1–2): Guardicore maps every flow in production network.
Visualization Phase (Week 3): You see your own network for the first time.
Policy Draft (Week 4): We generate a segmentation policy. No enforcement yet.
Review (Week 5–6): You verify the policy matches your intent.
```

The "no enforcement in the first 6 weeks" framing removes the single biggest objection from OT-environment managers: "you'll break our production."
