# Sweet Spot Profile

The sweet spot is the intersection where partner capability, vendor technology, and customer pain all converge. If any of the three is absent, the sales motion fails.

---

## The intersection

```
Partner capability (Axians)
        ↕
  SWEET SPOT
        ↕
Customer pain             ←→    Vendor technology (Guardicore)
(NIS2 + OT/IT)
```

**Partner capability:** Axians has OT/IT network security expertise, existing customer relationships in German manufacturing, and a technical team that can deploy and operate microsegmentation solutions.

**Vendor technology:** Akamai Guardicore Segmentation provides agentless microsegmentation that works across OT, hybrid, and cloud environments. Key differentiator for Mittelstand: operates without disrupting production networks.

**Customer pain:** German Mittelstand manufacturers face a specific combination: NIS2 compliance mandate (Article 21 requires network segmentation), OT/IT convergence risk, and a strong preference for solutions that don't require factory downtime to deploy.

---

## The sweet spot narrative

> A German manufacturer running Industry 4.0 processes has converged their OT and IT networks. Every PLC and production robot is now theoretically reachable from the corporate network. NIS2 requires them to demonstrate network segmentation to auditors by [deadline]. Traditional segmentation requires firewall rule changes that could disrupt production. Guardicore provides agentless microsegmentation: it maps the network, visualises traffic flows, and enforces segmentation policies without touching production configuration. Axians deploys it, integrates it into the customer's IT/OT environment, and operates the monitoring. The customer gets the NIS2 audit checkbox and the operational security improvement. Axians gets a new revenue stream. Akamai gets a reference customer.
