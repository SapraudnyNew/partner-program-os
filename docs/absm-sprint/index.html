Perspective 3

ABSM Partner Enablement

A complete Account-Based Sales & Marketing program built for Axians, targeting four German Mittelstand manufacturers. Everything ready-made — the partner's job is to meet clients.

In this section
  • Targeting — ICP definition, scoring matrix, 30-company longlist narrowed to 4 final accounts
  • Strategy — sweet-spot profile, pain-pattern library, content matrix, competitive positioning
  • Client deep-dives — Hörmann, Reinhausen, Witte, Trumpf (company brief, pain map, relationship map, Axians connection per account)
  • Collateral — 12 branded execution PDFs ready for partner handoff
  • Infrastructure — CRM spec (HubSpot), KPI dashboard, MDF allocation model, launch checklist

The operating principle

Most partner enablement programs create content libraries and training modules, then wait. The partner is supposed to figure out how to use them. This program inverts the approach.

Instead of tools and hope, the partner gets ready-made account kits. One per target customer. Research done. Pain points mapped. Messaging written. Competitive positioning prepared. The partner's only job is to walk into the meeting.

The program was designed for Axians as the template partner, from the Pursue Five priority list. The structure is replicable: same framework, any partner, any territory, any product line. Four target accounts were selected from Axians' customer base through a systematic scoring process. Each gets a complete execution package: company profile, pain-pattern map, talk track, and competitive battlecard. All branded. All formatted for partner handoff.

The supporting infrastructure ships with it: HubSpot CRM field mapping, KPI dashboard for pipeline tracking, MDF allocation model, and a launch checklist that sequences the first 12 weeks. Built to run, not to present.

The AI angle: personalisation at scale

The entire ABSM pipeline, from company research through collateral production to CRM data entry, runs on AI agents. What takes a traditional channel marketing team 6 to 8 weeks per partner gets done in days. This is how five to ten partners get enabled simultaneously without proportional headcount growth. The speed comes from the toolchain. The quality comes from the methodology.

Why this matters

The ABSM program is a proof of concept for a partner enablement operating model. Instead of asking partners to figure out their own go-to-market, the channel marketing function delivers complete campaign kits — per partner, per account — with every artifact needed to walk into a meeting prepared. The AI pipeline makes this scalable: what used to take a team weeks per partner now takes days, which means the channel marketing manager can run parallel enablement for multiple partners simultaneously without proportional headcount growth.

For Axians specifically, the program targets four German Mittelstand manufacturers who sit at the intersection of NIS2 compliance pressure, OT/IT convergence, and a demonstrable gap in microsegmentation. Each account was selected through a systematic scoring process, not because "they seem interesting." The rationale is auditable.

All company research is a first pass based on publicly available information, prepared for demonstration of methodology. Account dossiers, pain maps, and relationship maps should be validated with partner and customer data before any external engagement.
