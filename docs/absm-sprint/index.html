Perspective 3

ABSM Partner Enablement

A complete Account-Based Sales & Marketing program built for Axians, targeting four German Mittelstand manufacturers. Everything ready-made — the partner's job is to meet clients.

The operating principle

Most partner enablement programs create content libraries and training modules, then wait for partners to figure out how to use them. This inverts the approach. Instead of giving partners tools and hoping they sell, the program gives partners ready-made account kits — one per target customer — with the research already done, the pain points already mapped, the messaging already written, and the competitive positioning already prepared. The partner's only job is to walk into the meeting.

The program was designed for Axians as the template partner (from the Pursue Five priority list). The structure is replicable — the same framework can be deployed for any partner, any territory, any product line. The four target accounts were selected from Axians' existing customer base using a systematic scoring process described below.

The AI angle: personalization at scale

The ABSM pipeline uses AI agents at every stage — from company research and pain-point extraction to collateral generation and CRM data entry. The entire program (ICP definition → target scoring → account research → collateral production → CRM setup) was built as a multi-agent pipeline. What takes a traditional channel marketing team 6–8 weeks to produce for one partner can be generated in days. This is how you enable 5–10 partners simultaneously without growing headcount proportionally.

Why this matters

The ABSM program is a proof of concept for a partner enablement operating model. Instead of asking partners to figure out their own go-to-market, the channel marketing function delivers complete campaign kits — per partner, per account — with every artifact needed to walk into a meeting prepared. The AI pipeline makes this scalable: what used to take a team weeks per partner now takes days, which means the channel marketing manager can run parallel enablement for multiple partners simultaneously without proportional headcount growth.

For Axians specifically, the program targets four German Mittelstand manufacturers who sit at the intersection of NIS2 compliance pressure, OT/IT convergence, and a demonstrable gap in microsegmentation. Each account was selected through a systematic scoring process, not because "they seem interesting." The rationale is auditable.

All company research is a first pass based on publicly available information, prepared for demonstration of methodology. Account dossiers, pain maps, and relationship maps should be validated with partner and customer data before any external engagement.
