Scorecard Template

The full 7×3 maturity matrix. Each stage is scored across three levels using binary capability checkpoints and measurable KPIs. This template can be applied to any B2B partner program.

Stages
  • 1. Recruit
  • 2. Onboard
  • 3. Enable
  • 4. Co-Sell
  • 5. Deliver
  • 6. Renew
  • 7. Expand

The scoring approach is binary at the checkpoint level: each capability is either present or absent. A stage is scored at the highest level where all checkpoints are satisfied. If a program meets all Basic checkpoints and three of five Professional checkpoints, the stage score is Basic — Professional is not achieved until all Professional checkpoints are met.

01 Recruit
Basic
  • Partner program exists with defined tier structure
  • Partner portal operational with self-registration
  • Partner types defined (reseller, SI, MSSP, distributor)
  • At least one active partner in the region
KPI: New partners / quarter ≥ 1 KPI: Time-to-sign ≤ 90 days
Professional
  • Ideal Partner Profile (IPP) defined with weighted scoring criteria
  • Structured partner pipeline with named targets and dispositions
  • Dedicated partner recruitment headcount or allocated capacity
  • Account-specific outreach with tailored value propositions
  • Competitive entanglement mapping completed
KPI: New partners / quarter ≥ 3 KPI: IPP match rate ≥ 80% KPI: Pipeline coverage ≥ 2× target
World-Class
  • Ecosystem-led referral mechanism producing inbound partner applications
  • Partner community driving peer-to-peer recruitment
  • GE/McKinsey prioritization framework applied to partner portfolio
  • Automated partner scoring with CRM/PRM integration
KPI: ≥ 30% of new partners from referral KPI: Cost per partner acquisition tracked and declining
02 Onboard
Basic
  • Global partner portal with self-serve onboarding resources
  • Certification path available
  • Deal registration system operational
KPI: Portal login rate ≥ 50% KPI: Time-to-first-deal tracked
Professional
  • Regional onboarding playbook (localized language, regulatory context)
  • Dedicated partner success manager assigned to new partners
  • Structured onboarding SLA with time-to-first-deal target
  • Named internal champion paired with each new partner
  • First customer targeting session within 30 days of signing
KPI: Time-to-first-deal ≤ 90 days KPI: Onboarding completion ≥ 85%
World-Class
  • Automated onboarding workflow triggered on contract signature
  • Personalized onboarding path based on partner type and capabilities
  • Real-time progress dashboard for both partner and vendor
  • Integration with vendor's CRM for seamless data flow
KPI: Time-to-first-deal ≤ 60 days KPI: 90-day churn ≤ 5%
03 Enable
Basic
  • Product training available (online or in-person)
  • Certification program active
  • MDF / co-marketing support exists
  • Competitive battlecards available
KPI: ≥ 1 certified rep per active partner KPI: Content downloads tracked
Professional
  • Regional channel marketing function staffed
  • Localized campaign calendar aligned to regional event cadence
  • Co-marketing campaigns executed with measurable pipeline attribution
  • Industry-specific messaging and use-case libraries
  • Regular enablement webinars or workshops (monthly+)
KPI: MDF utilization ≥ 70% KPI: Partner-sourced pipeline / vendor-assisted ≥ 0.5
World-Class
  • Self-service content library with customization tools
  • Partner generates pipeline independently without vendor hand-holding
  • Gamified certification with tiered incentives
  • AI-assisted content personalization for partner market segments
KPI: Partner-sourced pipeline / vendor-assisted ≥ 1.5 KPI: Self-serve content usage ≥ 60%
04 Co-Sell
Basic
  • Deal registration system operational
  • Co-sell incentives documented (rebates, deal protection)
  • GSP / cloud marketplace co-sell program active
KPI: Deal registrations / quarter tracked KPI: Partner-sourced pipeline exists
Professional
  • Joint pipeline review cadence (monthly with top partners)
  • Quarterly Business Reviews with named strategic partners
  • Named-account co-sell target lists per partner territory
  • Regional PAM team structure with assigned partner portfolios
  • Deal registration SLA consistently met (< 48h approval)
KPI: Partner-sourced pipeline ≥ 25% of regional target KPI: Deal-reg approval SLA met ≥ 90%
World-Class
  • Intent data routed to partners (account-level signals, GDPR-compliant)
  • Partner-facing account kits per intent signal (research brief, email sequence, case study)
  • Co-invested pipeline targets with shared risk/reward
  • Real-time partner pipeline visibility integrated into vendor CRM
KPI: Partner-sourced pipeline ≥ 40% of regional target KPI: Co-sell win rate ≥ 1.2× direct win rate
05 Deliver
Basic
  • Partner implementation capability exists
  • Vendor professional services available as backstop
  • Basic quality expectations communicated
KPI: Implementation CSAT tracked
Professional
  • Certification gates for partner delivery (e.g., GCSP-equivalent)
  • Tiered SLA structure for partner-delivered implementations
  • Partner delivery scorecard with regular reviews
  • Escalation path and remediation process defined
  • Subscription model aligns partner economics with delivery outcomes
KPI: Partner delivery CSAT ≥ 4.0/5.0 KPI: Escalation rate ≤ 10%
World-Class
  • Partner owns full delivery lifecycle autonomously
  • Vendor delivery team focuses on complex/strategic accounts only
  • Partner delivery quality is a competitive differentiator
  • Delivery feedback loop directly informs product roadmap
KPI: Partner delivery CSAT ≥ 4.5/5.0 KPI: ≥ 80% implementations partner-autonomous
06 Renew
Basic
  • Renewal process exists (manual or auto-renewal)
  • Partner receives renewal commission
  • Churn rate tracked at program level
KPI: Renewal rate tracked KPI: NRR ≥ 95%
Professional
  • Managed renewal cadence: 365/180/90-day reviews
  • At-risk account identification and intervention process
  • Expansion opportunity mapped at renewal point
  • Partner economics incentivize renewal equally to new business
  • NRR reported per partner and per product
KPI: NRR ≥ 105% KPI: At-risk accounts intervened ≥ 80%
World-Class
  • Predictive at-risk early warning system (usage data, health scores)
  • Automated expansion triggers at renewal (product usage → upsell recommendation)
  • Partner self-manages renewal pipeline with vendor oversight only
  • Multi-year contract structure with built-in expansion commitments
KPI: NRR ≥ 115% KPI: Renewal pipeline coverage ≥ 1.5×
07 Expand
Basic
  • Multi-product portfolio available through channel
  • Cross-sell / upsell incentives exist
  • Products-per-partner metric tracked
KPI: Avg products/partner ≥ 1.5
Professional
  • Partner account planning framework in place
  • White-space analysis completed per strategic partner
  • Portfolio expansion playbooks per product combination
  • Multi-product deal incentives with accelerated margins
  • Partner-led expansion pipeline tracked separately
KPI: Avg products/partner ≥ 2.5 KPI: Expansion revenue ≥ 15% of partner total
World-Class
  • Portfolio-orchestrated expansion: automated cross-sell recommendation engine
  • Partner specialization paths drive deep expertise across multiple products
  • New market entry campaigns run jointly with expansion partners
  • Partner advisory board informs portfolio expansion strategy
KPI: Avg products/partner ≥ 3.5 KPI: Expansion revenue ≥ 25% of partner total

How to use this scorecard

For each stage, work through the checkpoints top to bottom. Score the stage at the highest level where all checkpoints are satisfied. Record evidence for each checkpoint — what confirms it is present, or what confirms it is absent. Where evidence is unavailable (gated behind internal data), mark the checkpoint as "inferred" with the reasoning.

The resulting 7-digit score (e.g., B-B-B-B-P-P-B) produces a maturity profile that can be visualized on the radar chart. The shape of the radar reveals the program's signature: which stages carry the program and which are underbuilt. The gap between current state and target state becomes the roadmap.

This scorecard template is a general-purpose assessment tool. When applied to a specific company, all observations should be validated with internal operational data. Checkpoints marked "inferred" require insider verification.
