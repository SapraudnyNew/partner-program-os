Priority Recruitment Targets

Pursue Five

Five DACH cybersecurity partners selected for priority outreach based on 6-dimension scoring. Each has a specific structural opening and prepared outreach hook.

These five partners were selected from the 33-partner DACH landscape based on a combination of high product-gap fit, low competitive entanglement, NIS2-exposed customer bases, and identifiable structural openings. Each profile includes firmographics, vendor stack analysis, the specific outreach rationale, and a named-executive contact approach.

1. Axians Deutschland + Fernao
DE · ~4,000 FTE · €945M
VINCI Energies 65+ locations DE +15.3% YoY growth Fernao integration 2024–25

The largest DACH cybersecurity SI outside the hyperscaler-affiliated tier. Axians acquired Fernao Networks in 2024, creating a combined entity with ~4,000 employees, €945M revenue, and 65+ German locations. The security practice spans managed SOC, network security, identity/access management, and endpoint protection. Crucially, their stack has no microsegmentation play — Fortinet, Palo Alto, and Cisco for network security, CrowdStrike and Trend Micro for endpoint, but no Illumio or direct Guardicore competitor.

The Fernao integration creates a once-in-cycle opening: new vendor decisions are being made as the combined security portfolio is rationalised. VINCI Energies' decentralised operating model means the Axians IT Security unit in Leinfelden-Echterdingen has autonomous vendor selection authority.

Outreach Hook

Post-integration vendor portfolio rationalisation. The microsegmentation gap is visible. A Guardicore pilot positioned alongside their existing Fortinet/PANW network security stack fills a named gap without competing with existing vendor relationships.

2. AVANTEC AG
CH · 50+ security specialists
Zurich-based Swiss market leader Illumio newcomer tier Multi-vendor model

Swiss cybersecurity specialist with deep enterprise customer base across banking, pharma, and manufacturing. AVANTEC runs a multi-vendor model — they evaluate and recommend rather than push a single stack. Their Illumio relationship is at newcomer tier, meaning it is shallow and uncommitted. This creates a direct Guardicore POC opportunity: demonstrate microsegmentation capability in a head-to-head evaluation where the partner has not yet made a deep investment.

Outreach Hook

Illumio newcomer status = the evaluation window is still open. A Guardicore technical workshop for their security team, positioned as "giving you the comparison data to make the right recommendation to your customers," aligns with their evaluator identity.

3. SVA System Vertrieb Alexander
DE · ~1,800 FTE · Wiesbaden
Fortinet EPSP No Illumio Public sector + Mittelstand Owner-managed

One of Germany's largest independent IT system houses. SVA holds Fortinet Expert Partner (EPSP) status — strong on network security, firewall, and SD-WAN. Their portfolio has no microsegmentation capability: no Illumio, no Guardicore, no dedicated Zero Trust segmentation solution. Customer base spans public sector (Länder, municipalities) and upper-Mittelstand manufacturing — both NIS2-exposed segments.

As an owner-managed company (the Alexander family), decision-making is faster and vendor relationships more personal than at PE-backed competitors.

Outreach Hook

Guardicore complements their Fortinet stack without competing. The pitch: "Your Fortinet practice protects the perimeter. Guardicore protects what's inside when the perimeter fails. Together, that's a complete NIS2 segmentation story for your Mittelstand accounts."

4. ACP Gruppe
AT · DACH · SOC Austria
Austrian market leader No Illumio SOC without microsegmentation DACH expansion

Austria's leading IT system integrator with a growing DACH footprint. ACP operates a Security Operations Centre (SOC) that provides managed detection and response — but the SOC stack has no microsegmentation layer. Their Austrian market position means they serve the Austrian NIS2-affected enterprise segment (manufacturing, energy, critical infrastructure) directly. No Illumio relationship identified.

Outreach Hook

SOC without microsegmentation = incomplete Zero Trust story. Adding Guardicore visibility to their SOC gives them a differentiated managed service offering. The pitch targets the SOC leadership: "Your customers expect segmentation as part of managed security. This fills the gap."

5. InfoGuard AG
CH · 350 security experts
Existing Akamai partner Baar, Switzerland Expanding into Germany CSIRT capability

InfoGuard is already in the Akamai partner ecosystem — the opportunity is to deepen, not recruit. With 350 dedicated security professionals, they are one of Switzerland's largest pure-play cybersecurity firms. They operate a 24/7 CSIRT (Computer Security Incident Response Team) and have been expanding their German market presence. The existing Akamai relationship means the onboarding friction is zero — this is an activation play, not a recruitment play.

Outreach Hook

Lowest-friction first activation. Already a partner. Conversation: "You already know Akamai. Here's what a managed Guardicore microsegmentation service looks like for your CSIRT customers. We'll co-fund the first pilot." Their Germany expansion makes them the ideal bridge partner between CH and DE.
