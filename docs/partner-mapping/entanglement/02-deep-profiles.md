## Reading guide

Each profile follows the same shape: identifiers, ownership/financials, vendor stack and tier evidence, executive landscape, regulatory/vertical posture, the *entanglement summary* in ADR-011 four-layer notation, and the Recruitability score with rationale. Profiles are ordered to surface displacement risk first (Illumio anchors), then the structurally locked anchors that Akamai must work *around*, then the Mittelstand recruitment candidates.
