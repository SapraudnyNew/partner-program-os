Akamai DACH Partner Deep Profiles

Version: v1 — 2026-05-18
Owner: D2-RC (Channel Intelligence)
Companion to: 01-entanglement-matrix.md
Profile target: Decision-grade dossiers for Pursue / Contain / Monitor / Drop disposition reviews.
Confidence levels: ✅ public primary · ⚠️ public secondary · 🧠 inferred · ❌ unverified

Reading guide

Each profile follows the same shape: identifiers, ownership/financials, vendor stack and tier evidence, executive landscape, regulatory/vertical posture, the entanglement summary in ADR-011 four-layer notation, and the Recruitability score with rationale. Profiles are ordered to surface displacement risk first (Illumio anchors), then the structurally locked anchors that Akamai must work around, then the Mittelstand recruitment candidates.

A. Illumio anchors — displacement targets (Drop / Contain)

  • HQ: Glogauer Straße 5, 10999 Berlin.
  • Leadership: Sven Launspach (CEO, also CBO smao GmbH); Samir Omar (CMO + Co-Founder, Head of Communications).

  • Vendor stack with tier evidence:

  • ✅ Illumio Radiate Status — “one of few listed Illumio partners in DACH” (Samir Omar LinkedIn).
  • ✅ Cloudflare Authorized Service Delivery Partner (ASDP) Zero Trust — since 2024.
  • ✅ Cloudflare Magic Network Services Partner — Dec-2024, second company worldwide to attain status.
  • ✅ Equinix Reseller Partner; Cisco Meraki + Umbrella reseller; Gridscale Managed SD-WAN.
  • 🧠 Participates in Gaia-X Project Tellus consortium (DE-CIX-led, with IONOS, Trumpf, PlusServer, Spacenet, Cloud&Heat).
  • Akamai presence: None. Not listed in Akamai DACH partner directory.
