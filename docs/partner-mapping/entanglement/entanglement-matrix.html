Entanglement Matrix — DACH Cybersecurity Partners

Scope: 33 DACH system integrators and MSSPs assessed across 5 competing vendors
Vendors mapped: Akamai (Guardicore), Illumio, Palo Alto Networks, Cisco, Zscaler
Entanglement levels: Deep (Partner of Year / Platinum) · Mid (Gold / Named) · Light (Select / Newcomer) · None

Reading this matrix

Entanglement depth predicts recruitability. A partner at Deep level with Illumio is structurally locked — the economic, technical, and reputational investment is too large to displace. A partner at Light or None with Illumio is a genuine opportunity.

The matrix focuses on Illumio entanglement as the primary competitive signal for Guardicore positioning. Palo Alto, Cisco, and Zscaler entanglement is tracked but does not create the same type of conflict (Guardicore is complementary to network security vendors).

Key findings

Zero Illumio entanglement: Axians+Fernao, SVA, ACP Gruppe — these three have no Illumio relationship at all. Combined, they represent ~€2.2B in IT services revenue and thousands of NIS2-exposed customers.

Structurally locked (Drop): KAEMI and Navixia both hold first-tier Illumio certifications (EMEA Partner of Year and first EMEA ZTS Professional respectively). These are not outreach opportunities — they are competitive intelligence subjects.

Computacenter anomaly: Computacenter holds Illumio MSP status but is currently at Akamai Select tier — significantly under-tiered relative to their DACH footprint (£5B global revenue). This is a Contain play, not a Pursue: the task is upgrading an existing relationship, not recruiting.
