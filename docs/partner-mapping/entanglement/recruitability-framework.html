Recruitability Framework

Recruitability Framework

A partner’s theoretical fit score tells you who you want. The recruitability framework tells you who you can actually get right now.

The six sub-criteria

Each criterion is scored 0–2. Total maximum = 12. Recruitability threshold: ≥ 7.

# Sub-criterion 0 1 2
R1 Illumio entanglement Deep (PoY / Platinum) Mid (Gold / named) Light or None
R2 Vendor portfolio openness Single-vendor locked Mixed but primary committed Genuinely multi-vendor
R3 Structural opening No change Organic growth Acquisition / reorg / leadership change
R4 Economic incentive fit No margin expansion possible Moderate uplift Clear margin uplift from adding Guardicore
R5 Customer NIS2 exposure <20% customers affected 20–60% affected >60% customers affected
R6 Geographic reach Single city Regional DACH-wide or national

When to re-evaluate

Monitor-tier partners move to Pursue when their R1 or R3 score changes:
- R1 changes: Illumio drops them from a tier, or a public break occurs (rare but happens)
- R3 changes: Acquisition by a new entity, new CEO, entry into a new geographic market, loss of a large customer that changes their product priority

Check Monitor-tier partners every 6 months. Check Contain-tier partners every 3 months (they are close enough to activate; timing is the variable).

Framework derived from direct channel management experience and calibrated against DACH market research.
