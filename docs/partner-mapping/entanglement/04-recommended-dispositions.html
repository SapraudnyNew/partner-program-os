2. Pursue — priority five (next 90 days)

P1. Axians Deutschland + Fernao — DE, Mannheim group HQ

Recruitability 4. Top Mittelstand recruit. Drivers: ISG Leader DE 2025 × 4 cyber categories; no flagship competitor lock at L1 or L2; mid-Fernao integration creates window for new vendor adds; explicit NIS2/DORA/KRITIS positioning that aligns to Guardicore + API Security wedge.

Next 30 days:
- Outbound from Akamai DACH Channel Director to Axians DE security lead. Target meeting: senior cyber strategy + ISG-Leader practice owner.
- Issue Guardicore + NIS2 micro-segmentation co-marketing brief (DE language).
- Identify 3 named-account triangulation targets where Axians is incumbent IT and the customer has visible KRITIS/NIS2 obligation.

Days 31-60: First joint named-account opportunity registration. POC scope: east-west micro-segmentation for OT segment in a manufacturing or energy KRITIS account.

Days 61-90: First registered deal in CRM. Co-marketed webinar on NIS2-Article 21 micro-segmentation requirements with Axians + Akamai branding.

Watch: Vinci Energies’ larger ICT consolidation moves (Axians vs. Actemium vs. SDEL); Fernao senior-team retention beyond the Oct-2025 brand consolidation.

P2. SVA — DE, Wiesbaden

Recruitability 3. Federal Mittelstand recruit. Drivers: Federal Business POY (PANW DE 2025), no public Akamai or Illumio, Fortinet EPSP exclusive lock applies to firewall/SD-WAN segment only — Guardicore micro-segmentation is orthogonal.

Next 30 days:
- Approach via federal vertical (BSI / Bundeswehr / KRITIS Bund). Map intersection with Akamai federal account list (where Akamai already has Guardicore federal traction).
- Confirm SVA has no existing Akamai partner agreement; if Select, set Premier-upgrade target.

Days 31-60: Joint federal SOC briefing day in Wiesbaden, alongside SVA’s existing federal-business team.

Days 61-90: First federal named-account POC.

Watch: Fortinet EPSP “first & only DE” exclusive may produce vendor-marketing pressure on SVA to keep parallel flagships off the press; structure Akamai PR accordingly.

P3. ACP Gruppe — AT, Vienna group HQ (covers AT + DE + CH)

Recruitability 3. Austrian-market beachhead. Drivers: 2,600+ employees, manufacturer-independent stated philosophy, Cyber Trust Label Gold (Austrian state-recognised), SOC Austria by ACP + NOC by ACP managed services. PANW is named partner but tier not Diamond-Innovator; multi-vendor by design.

Next 30 days:
- AT-field engagement: Akamai DACH AE assigned to ACP Wien.
- Position Guardicore for OT / KRITIS NIS2-AT-implementation (Austrian implementation under finalisation 2026).

Days 31-60: First Austrian named-account opportunity registration.

Days 61-90: Joint Austrian customer event (it-sa-AT or KRITIS NIS2 working session) with Guardicore demo.

P4. AVANTEC AG — CH, Zürich

Recruitability 4. Swiss displacement target. Drivers: Illumio sits at “newcomer” tier (not flagship), best-of-breed stated philosophy, 250 customers in CH, recent Netskope + BeyondTrust POY CEE recognitions show vendor-flex pattern.

Next 30 days:

- Co-funded labs day at AVANTEC HQ Heinrichstrasse 267.

Days 31-60: First Akamai POC in AVANTEC’s lab; named-account introduction via AVANTEC’s 250-customer base.

Days 61-90: Conditional graduation of Guardicore into AVANTEC’s main portfolio at the next quarterly review.

Watch: AVANTEC’s BeyondTrust POY CEE 2026 shows the CEE field-marketing motion is alive; align Akamai CEE field accordingly so AVANTEC sees Akamai as a peer-priority vendor.

Recruitability 3. Existing Akamai partner — deepen motion, not recruit motion. Drivers: 350+ security experts, 1,000+ customers across CH/DE/AT, BSI-qualified APT-Response, public Akamai (Guardicore) + PANW + Cisco partner pages already live.

Next 30 days:

Watch: InfoGuard’s Vienna + Frankfurt + Munich + Düsseldorf footprint overlaps with our Pursue partners (ACP, AVANTEC, Axians) — avoid channel conflict by named-account allocation.

5. Contain — work-around plan

For the 10 Contain partners (Computacenter, Bechtle, Cancom, K-Businesscom, Controlware, NTT Data DE, Indevis, Navixia, NTS AG, Deutsche Telekom Security for SASE/ZTNA), the playbook is named-account allocation, not vendor recruitment.

Standing rules:
- No Premier-tier upgrade investments. Keep Select tier where present.
- Deal-registration discipline: Akamai’s named-account map should specify which Contain partners are pre-cleared for which named accounts, by product line and by region.
- Field-marketing budget for Contain partners is allocated to joint customer events at the named-account level (not regional or vendor-led).
- Quarterly review of Contain partners for any tripwire that would re-open Pursue evaluation (see File 3 §6).

Specific Contain plays worth noting:

  • Computacenter: Co-sell only for Guardicore east-west micro-segmentation in named manufacturing / financial-services accounts where CC is incumbent SI but Zscaler ZIA and PANW Prisma Cloud do not natively cover east-west. Markus Kut (Unit Director Security Solution Management) is the documented Akamai-relevant decision-maker.
  • Bechtle: Limit DE motion to product-gap deals; direct the Bechtle Schweiz route for any CH Guardicore deal where Bechtle Schweiz already has Akamai partner-page activation.
  • Cancom: Approach the K-Businesscom (CANCOM Austria) angle for Austrian new-territory cyber-defense projects where the CANCOM Cyber Defense Center (Cortex-built) has OT / KRITIS coverage gaps.
  • Controlware: NIS2 co-author narrative is the maximum joint-marketing posture; no Diamond-tier ambitions.

9. Risks and open questions

R1. Computacenter–Illumio relationship nature. ADR-011’s “Illumio investor” framing is unsupported by Illumio’s public funding history. The relationship is documented as MSP/services and co-exhibitor at IWT 2025 Germany. ADR-011 should be amended; the true relationship nature (MSP vs. equity vs. board) materially affects whether Computacenter is reachable for Akamai-led deals.

R2. Cloudflare MSP exclusive (Sep-2025). Infinigate’s exclusive Cloudflare MSP role in DE puts the Akamai-edge story under direct mindshare pressure within the MSP channel. Without a matched Akamai MSP enablement programme, share-of-pipe is at risk in 2026.

R3. Cisco 360 PVI re-tiering (effective Jan-2026). The new Cisco Partner Value Index replaces Select/Premier/Gold with portfolio-specific “Preferred” badges across Networking / Security / Collaboration / Cloud&AI / Splunk / Services. NTS AG already qualified Multi-Preferred across all 6; Computacenter and Cancom are in similar position. The re-tiering may concentrate Cisco’s marketing/development funds further into Multi-Preferred partners, structurally widening their Cisco lock.

R4. PANW partner-program direction. PANW DE 2025 awards skew heavily to existing Diamond Innovators (Bechtle, Cancom) and a small set of GSIs (NTT Data, Computacenter via Premier). No partner has been added at Diamond tier since 2023. If PANW continues to consolidate share among existing top partners, the Pursue Mittelstand recruits (SVA, Axians, Nomios) face less PANW competitive pressure in the next 12 months — Akamai’s window of opportunity.

R5. Illumio Enlighten Partner Program evolution (Nov-2024 expansion). Spark / Radiate tiers + ZTS Select / Professional / Elite certifications widen Illumio’s partner surface area. Akamai should map this expansion at quarterly cadence to ensure no new DACH partner reaches Radiate or ZTS Professional without being captured in this matrix.

R6. Switzerland NIS2-equivalent timing. Swiss federal regulation under consultation; Akamai’s CH go-to-market plan via AVANTEC / InfoGuard / Kudelski Security depends on the regulatory tailwind landing in 2026-2027.

R7. DACH-specific Akamai Channel Director succession (Nadine Anders → Kong Inc. Feb-2025). Akamai’s own DACH channel leadership succession is documented; ensure the new role-holder’s named-partner introductions are aligned with this matrix’s Pursue list within the new owner’s first 90 days.
