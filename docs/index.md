# Partner Program OS

A working framework for designing, executing, and evolving partner programs in B2B environments.

---

## Why this exists

Partner programs in B2B fail in predictable ways. The framework underneath this work catalogs the patterns and the interventions that change them.

The work draws from a synthesis of contemporary partnership and ecosystem literature, applied to manufacturer-led partner ecosystems in European markets.
