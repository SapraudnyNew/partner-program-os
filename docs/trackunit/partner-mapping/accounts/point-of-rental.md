# Account Plan: Point of Rental Software

## Account snapshot

Point of Rental Software is a rental and inventory management vendor, effectively an ERP for rental businesses, with a product family spanning Expert and Elite (US flagships), Essentials, the UK-origin Syrinx and Syrinx 365, the Point of Rental One app, and the new Rental Intelligence Suite. The company is headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas, runs offices in the UK, Australia, South Africa and Canada, and employs roughly 305 people across five continents. Its customer base covers 5,000+ business locations in about 80 countries across equipment, event, tool, portable sanitation and specialty rental verticals. Ownership is private, backed by Susquehanna Growth Equity, and 2025 marked a tenth consecutive year on the Inc. 5000 list. The account intersects IrisX directly: a formal Trackunit partnership since January 2025 runs a two-way API between Expert and Elite and Trackunit Manager, and an ERP Connector for Syrinx 365 is already live in the Trackunit Marketplace, so every syncing seat drives Iris/IrisX API traffic today. That makes Point of Rental one of the clearest credit-consumption channels among rental ERPs and the template account for the Deepen play.

## Why now

- **January 2025**: long-term Trackunit partnership announced, a bidirectional API between Point of Rental Expert and Elite and Trackunit Manager plus the Trackunit Go app, syncing on-rent and off-rent status, delivery events and billing data. CEO Wayne Harris framed it as taking IoT to the next level with Trackunit.
- **January 2025**: first-ever CTO hired, Emad Georgy, with an AI background, following the company's first CFO in August 2024. (Inference from the web digest: PE-backed professionalization of the executive layer and a pivot from record-keeping software toward an AI and data platform, which raises the value of machine-data feeds like Trackunit's.)
- **March 1, 2026**: acquired The Owl, a rental data insights platform, its most recent deal per PitchBook, with the analytics folded into the Rental Intelligence Suite.
- **March 2, 2026**: launched the Rental Intelligence Suite at the ARA Show in Orlando, described as the first agentic intelligence layer built for rental operations: natural-language dashboards, utilization and ROI analytics, cross-fleet benchmarks, nightly AI insights and background agents, trained on de-identified data from thousands of rental companies. An AI-powered Intelligent Phone Agent launched the same day.
- **Ongoing**: parallel telematics partnerships with Hapn (preferred GPS partner for Elite customers) and Matrix iQ (asset tracking for Syrinx 365 customers in the UK) show telematics is a multi-vendor category here, so integration depth is the competitive lever for Trackunit.

## Integration angle and entry path

The play is Deepen: the partnership is signed, the two-way API between Expert and Elite and Trackunit Manager works, and a Syrinx 365 ERP Connector already sits in the Trackunit Marketplace. What is missing publicly is a marketplace-grade connector for Expert and Elite, a joint go-to-market motion into the 5,000+ business locations, and consumption instrumentation, meaning per-tenant activation, pull frequency and credit reporting. On IrisX surfaces: the Rental ERP API carries the two-way contract, status and billing sync that is the core of the connector; GraphQL serves the portal and dashboard reads; Time Series feeds meter and utilization data into the Rental Intelligence Suite's analytics and benchmarks; MCP is the forward option once Point of Rental's background AI agents need to query fleet data conversationally (inference on timing). The sequence of first conversations by role: start with the VP of Payments & Ecosystem to frame the connector, the marketplace listing and one co-marketing motion; bring in the VP of Platform & Data Science and the Head of AI on the Time Series feed into the Rental Intelligence Suite; take connector scope and product-line extension to the SVP of Global Product Management and the CTO; run the EMEA track through the UK Managing Director using Syrinx 365 activation data as proof. Since no partnerships director exists on the Point of Rental side, the ecosystem VP and the product org jointly play that role, and the plan should not wait for a partner-team counterpart to appear.

## First 30 days and main risk

- Pull internal Trackunit data on the existing integration: which Expert, Elite and Syrinx 365 tenants sync today, at what pull frequency, and what credit consumption that already represents. This is the baseline dashboard the Deepen thesis needs.
- Request a joint review with Point of Rental, ecosystem and product leadership together, on activation numbers and the gap between locations sold and locations syncing.
- Scope the marketplace-grade connector for Expert and Elite, matching the pattern of the existing RentalMan and Baseplan ERP connectors, with self-service activation as the design goal.
- Agree one concrete co-marketing motion into the Point of Rental user base, for example a joint session at their user conference or a campaign to mutual customers (assumption on format, to be negotiated).
- Draft the Time Series proposal for the Rental Intelligence Suite: which IrisX data fields enrich utilization, ROI and repair-cost analytics, as the expansion story beyond status sync.
- Document everything as the reusable Deepen template for inspHire, MCS, Texada and Wynne.
- Main risk: Point of Rental sells its own Hapn telematics bundle as preferred GPS partner for Elite, so deepening Trackunit competes with an in-house revenue line. Mitigation: position IrisX for mixed OEM fleets that Hapn hardware does not cover, and lead with the Rental Intelligence Suite data angle, where breadth of normalized machine data is the value and Hapn is not a substitute.
