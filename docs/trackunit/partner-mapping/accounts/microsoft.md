# Account Plan: Microsoft

## Account snapshot

Microsoft Corporation, headquartered in Redmond, Washington, is a public company (NASDAQ: MSFT) with roughly 228,000 employees. FY2025 revenue reached $281.7B, up 15%, with operating income of $128.5B and Azure passing $75B, up 34%. Three parts of this giant matter for IrisX: the commercial marketplace and ISV programs inside Global Partner Solutions, the Dynamics 365 Field Service and Finance & Operations products inside Business Applications engineering, and the Manufacturing & Mobility industry cloud team, which is where construction lives since Microsoft has no separate construction industry cloud (inference from Microsoft's industry site structure). The play is one relationship covering two consumption lanes: Dynamics 365 ERP connectors for rental and field-service transactions, and the Copilot agent channel that Trackunit's MCP Server already targets. Microsoft bills partner agents through Copilot Credits, a consumption model that mirrors the IrisX credit metric, so the commercial narratives on both sides already speak the same language. No Trackunit listing exists today on AppSource or Azure Marketplace, and no formal Trackunit-Microsoft partnership has been announced, so everything below is greenfield.

## Why now

- September 2025: Azure Marketplace and AppSource merged into a single Microsoft Marketplace with one Partner Center listing flow and 6M+ monthly unique visitors; a dedicated AI Apps category launched in 2026.
- November 2025 (Ignite), launching 2026: App Accelerate unifies ISV Success, Marketplace Rewards and co-sell resources into one pathway, and early co-sell access is no longer gated only by the $100K revenue threshold. Lower barriers for a first-time Microsoft ISV like Trackunit.
- April to September 2026: Dynamics 365 release wave 1 is explicitly agentic, and D365 Finance adds support for rental-based business models, tracking rental assets, revenue and related processes. This lands directly on Trackunit's rental-company customer base (inference on the fit, fact on the release plan). Field Service gets a Scheduling Operations Agent and deeper ties to Finance & Operations.
- After October 30, 2025: several legacy Field Service IoT integrations were deprecated, while Field Service continues to support custom IoT providers. The deprecations create whitespace for a construction telematics platform to become the equipment-data provider into Field Service work orders (inference).
- Through 2026: MCP support went GA in Copilot Studio and for declarative agents in M365 Copilot, and Copilot monetization shifted to consumption via Copilot Credits, with ISV agents distributed through the Agent Store and published via Partner Center.

## Integration angle and entry path

One Microsoft relationship covers two IrisX consumption surfaces. Lane one is ERP: IrisX Rental ERP API and GraphQL feeds into Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations rental and invoicing workflows, plus IrisX Time Series and machine events pushed into Field Service as a custom IoT provider, filling the whitespace left by the 2025 deprecations of legacy Field Service integrations. Lane two is agents: the Trackunit MCP Server, already built and already naming Copilot, published through Copilot Studio's MCP connector infrastructure and distributed via the Agent Store, where every natural-language fleet question becomes an IrisX credit event. The sequence of first conversations by role: first the EMEA ISV technology strategist to validate the MCP-to-Copilot-Studio architecture and the Marketplace listing path under ISV Success or App Accelerate; second the EMEA partner sales senior director to attach a commercial owner and a co-sell ambition; third the global strategic ISV and Data & AI partnership directors to position Trackunit inside the AI Apps category; fourth the industry co-sell lead to route the story into Manufacturing & Mobility field teams; in parallel, the D365 AI ERP product manager to test the fit with the 2026 rental-business-model features. The longlist contains no D365 Field Service partner contact and no Business Applications ISV PDM, so the plan must open that specific door by referral through the ISV partnership contacts rather than directly, and it should say so honestly (the roles exist per Microsoft's own job postings, the scan just did not surface a named person).

## First 30 days and main risk

- Register Trackunit in Partner Center and enroll in ISV Success; map the exact requirements for a transactable Microsoft Marketplace listing and for App Accelerate eligibility when it opens.
- Confirm internally the state of the Copilot connection of the MCP Server (listed as coming soon in Trackunit support material) and scope what Copilot Studio GA MCP support requires: auth, VNet, DLP controls.
- Book the first EMEA conversation with the Danish ISV technology strategist; prepare a one-page architecture showing IrisX MCP into Copilot Studio and IrisX Rental ERP API into D365 F&O.
- Ask the D365 rental ISV layer (STAEDEAN, Sycor, HSO, covered in separate dossiers) which Microsoft PDMs they work with, to triangulate the missing Business Applications ISV PDM name.
- Define the credit-consumption instrumentation for both lanes before any listing goes live, so the partnership reports IrisX credits from day one.
- Main risk: Microsoft's scale produces slow motion and generic treatment; a small Danish ISV can wait quarters for a co-sell decision. Mitigation: do not gate anything on Microsoft. Ship the Marketplace listing and the MCP-to-Copilot connection through self-service programs (ISV Success, Partner Center) on Trackunit's own timeline, and use the D365 rental ISVs as the fast-moving ERP channel while the direct Microsoft relationship matures.
