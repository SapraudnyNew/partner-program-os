Execution: Pod, Plan, Marketplace

The method and the mapping answer what to build and with whom. This section answers how it gets built, adopted, and measured. Four artifacts, each one operational on day one.

What is here

First Five Integrations: Execution Plan
The five priority partners from the mapping taken through Scope, Build, Launch, and Adopt. Per partner: the play, the scoping summary, a commercial model sketch, the path to first credit with reference timelines, the adoption motion, and the main risk. Ends with a combined quarter view and the logic of compounding integrations into reusable marketplace connectors.

Pod Operating Plan: The First 90 Days
How the cross-functional pod runs: operating principles, decision rights for every role, the weekly cadence with structured interlocks into P&E and GTM, a KPI tree rooted in IrisX credit consumption, and 30/60/90 milestones. Written to be argued with in the first week, not admired.

Marketplace Opportunity Map
What is already strong in the IrisX marketplace and developer surface, benchmarked against Procore, Salesforce AppExchange, Shopify, Autodesk, and Samsara. Seven leverage moves for low-touch ISV activation, each tied to its consumption effect and sized by effort.

Credit Consumption Dashboard
An interactive model of how the integration funnel converts into credit consumption: pipeline by stage, time-to-first-credit, per-connector run-rate, and a 12-month ramp by connector cohort. Indexed units. Every number is a labeled assumption because credit mechanics are not public.

The thread through all four

One metric at the root: IrisX credit consumption. The plan sources integrations that can drive it, the pod cadence inspects it weekly, the marketplace moves lower the cost of adding the next connector, and the dashboard makes the whole loop readable by a CFO.

First pass from public sources. Presented to demonstrate methodology, to be validated with internal data.
