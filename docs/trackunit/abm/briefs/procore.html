Trackunit Partner ABM · Executive brief

Procore

July 2026 · Public data, first pass

Snapshot

Procore (NYSE: PCOR) is a construction technology platform headquartered in Carpinteria, California, with roughly 4,200 employees, FY2025 revenue of about $1.3B, and FY2026 guidance raised to $1.499B to $1.53B after Q1 2026 revenue of $359.3M, up 16% year over year. It runs an App Marketplace of roughly 539 apps and a tiered Technology Partner Program. Its Equipment Telematics feature is an open API that today ingests Caterpillar, John Deere, Samsara and United Rentals data. No Trackunit connection is listed: the mixed-fleet source is missing.

Why now

  • February 26, 2026: United Rentals strategic partnership, Procore's first telematics-centered partnership, syncing rental fleet data into Resource Management. Proof Procore takes third-party fleet data natively.
  • January 20, 2026: Datagrid acquisition completed; its CEO now leads AI and data strategy at Procore. Equipment telematics is an obvious next data silo for agents to consume (inference).
  • June 16, 2025: tiered Technology Partner Program announced, rewarding partners on joint customer value. A formal on-ramp now exists.

The integration

A whitespace connector: Procore's Equipment Telematics API lists four per-OEM sources and no OEM-agnostic feed. IrisX normalizes 100+ OEM integrations, exactly what a contractor with a mixed fleet needs in one Procore view. IrisX surfaces: GraphQL for machine location, hours and status snapshots into the Equipment Telematics API; Time Series for the recurring utilization pulls that Resource Planning and Helix recommendations would consume; MCP as the forward story for Procore's agent strategy, where every agent query about a machine is an IrisX call (inference on agent volume).

First 30 days

  • Confirm Procore's telematics partner requirements from the public developer documentation and the Technology Partner Program materials.
  • Build a demo feed against the Equipment Telematics open API using IrisX GraphQL, showing one mixed fleet (Cat plus non-Cat machines) in a single Procore view.
  • Ask Trackunit sales for mutual customers: contractors running both Procore and Trackunit are the warm path into Procore's partner team.
  • Open the EMEA partnerships conversation with the mutual-customer evidence, and brief the global partnerships organization through the partner program's formal intake.

Main risk

Procore treats telematics sources as commodity feeds and offers a directory listing without co-marketing, which caps activation and credit consumption. Mitigation: lead with mixed-fleet coverage breadth that Cat and Deere structurally cannot offer, tied to the Helix and agent roadmap.
