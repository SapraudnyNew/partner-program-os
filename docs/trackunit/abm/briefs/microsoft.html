Trackunit Partner ABM · Executive brief

Microsoft

July 2026 · Public data, first pass

Snapshot

Microsoft (NASDAQ: MSFT), headquartered in Redmond, Washington, has roughly 228,000 employees; FY2025 revenue reached $281.7B, up 15%, with Azure passing $75B. Three parts matter for IrisX: the commercial marketplace and ISV programs inside Global Partner Solutions, the Dynamics 365 Field Service and Finance & Operations products, and the Manufacturing & Mobility industry cloud, where construction lives (inference). Microsoft bills partner agents through Copilot Credits, a consumption model that mirrors the IrisX credit metric. No Trackunit listing exists today on AppSource or Azure Marketplace: everything here is greenfield.

Why now

  • September 2025: Azure Marketplace and AppSource merged into a single Microsoft Marketplace with one Partner Center listing flow and 6M+ monthly unique visitors; a dedicated AI Apps category launched in 2026.
  • November 2025 (Ignite), launching 2026: App Accelerate unifies ISV Success, Marketplace Rewards and co-sell resources into one pathway, with early co-sell access no longer gated only by the $100K revenue threshold.
  • April to September 2026: Dynamics 365 release wave 1 is explicitly agentic, and D365 Finance adds support for rental-based business models. MCP support went GA in Copilot Studio, with ISV agents distributed through the Agent Store.

The integration

One relationship, two consumption lanes. Lane one, ERP: IrisX Rental ERP API and GraphQL feeds into Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations rental and invoicing workflows, plus IrisX Time Series and machine events pushed into Field Service as a custom IoT provider, filling the whitespace left by the 2025 deprecations of legacy Field Service IoT integrations. Lane two, agents: the Trackunit MCP Server, already built and already naming Copilot, published through Copilot Studio's MCP connector infrastructure and distributed via the Agent Store, where every natural-language fleet question becomes an IrisX credit event.

First 30 days

  • Register Trackunit in Partner Center and enroll in ISV Success; map the exact requirements for a transactable Microsoft Marketplace listing and for App Accelerate eligibility.
  • Confirm internally the state of the Copilot connection of the MCP Server and scope what Copilot Studio GA MCP support requires: auth, VNet, DLP controls.
  • Book the first EMEA ISV partnerships conversation with a one-page architecture: IrisX MCP into Copilot Studio, IrisX Rental ERP API into D365 F&O.
  • Define the credit-consumption instrumentation for both lanes before any listing goes live, so the partnership reports IrisX credits from day one.

Main risk

Microsoft's scale produces slow motion and generic treatment; a small Danish ISV can wait quarters for a co-sell decision. Mitigation: do not gate anything on Microsoft; ship the listing and the MCP-to-Copilot connection through self-service programs, and use the D365 rental ISVs (STAEDEAN, Sycor, HSO) as the fast-moving ERP channel.
