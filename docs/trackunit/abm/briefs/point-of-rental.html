Trackunit Partner ABM · Executive brief

Point of Rental Software

July 2026 · Public data, first pass

Snapshot

Point of Rental Software, headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas, is a rental and inventory management vendor with a product family spanning Expert and Elite (US flagships), Essentials, the UK-origin Syrinx 365, and the new Rental Intelligence Suite. Roughly 305 employees serve 5,000+ business locations in about 80 countries; ownership is private, backed by Susquehanna Growth Equity. The account intersects IrisX directly: a formal Trackunit partnership since January 2025 runs a two-way API between Expert and Elite and Trackunit Manager, and an ERP Connector for Syrinx 365 is live in the Trackunit Marketplace. This is the template account for the Deepen play.

Why now

  • January 2025: long-term Trackunit partnership announced, a bidirectional API syncing on-rent and off-rent status, delivery events and billing data. The same month, first-ever CTO hired, Emad Georgy, with an AI background.
  • March 1, 2026: acquired The Owl, a rental data insights platform, with the analytics folded into the Rental Intelligence Suite.
  • March 2, 2026: launched the Rental Intelligence Suite at the ARA Show, described as the first agentic intelligence layer built for rental operations: natural-language dashboards, utilization analytics, nightly AI insights and background agents.

The integration

Deepen what is already signed: build a marketplace-grade, self-service connector for Expert and Elite, add a joint go-to-market motion into the 5,000+ business locations, and instrument consumption (per-tenant activation, pull frequency, credit reporting). IrisX surfaces: the Rental ERP API carries the two-way contract, status and billing sync at the core of the connector; GraphQL serves portal and dashboard reads; Time Series feeds meter and utilization data into the Rental Intelligence Suite's analytics and benchmarks; MCP is the forward option once their background AI agents query fleet data conversationally (inference on timing).

First 30 days

  • Pull internal Trackunit data on the existing integration: which Expert, Elite and Syrinx 365 tenants sync today, at what pull frequency, and what credit consumption that already represents.
  • Request a joint review with Point of Rental ecosystem and product leadership on activation numbers and the gap between locations sold and locations syncing.
  • Scope the marketplace-grade connector for Expert and Elite, matching the pattern of the existing RentalMan and Baseplan ERP connectors, and agree one concrete co-marketing motion.
  • Draft the Time Series proposal for the Rental Intelligence Suite, and document everything as the reusable Deepen template for inspHire, MCS, Texada and Wynne.

Main risk

Point of Rental sells its own Hapn telematics bundle as preferred GPS partner for Elite, so deepening Trackunit competes with an in-house revenue line. Mitigation: position IrisX for mixed OEM fleets that Hapn hardware does not cover, and lead with the Rental Intelligence Suite data angle, where breadth of normalized machine data is the value.
