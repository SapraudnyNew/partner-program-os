Trackunit Partner ABM · Executive brief

Palantir Technologies

July 2026 · Public data, first pass

Snapshot

Palantir (NASDAQ: PLTR), headquartered in Denver, Colorado, had 4,429 full-time employees as of December 31, 2025. Q1 2026 revenue was $1.6B, up 85% year over year, with US commercial revenue up 133% and FY2026 guidance raised to about $7.65B. Construction is now a named vertical, with a "Palantir for Construction" offering page live and two flagship contractor deployments, Thomas Cavanagh Construction and McCarthy Building Companies. No classic reseller or alliances program exists on the commercial side, and Foundry ontologies for construction run on exactly the machine telemetry IrisX normalizes across 100+ OEM integrations. This is the labeled bold bet of the five plays.

Why now

  • Late May 2026: Thomas Cavanagh Construction extended its Foundry partnership to an 11-year term through December 31, 2035, and created Cavtera, a subsidiary commercializing its Foundry-built construction apps: a customer turning into a channel.
  • June 2026: McCarthy Building Companies signed a multi-year, multi-million dollar partnership; its AI-native field system "Pulse" was demoed at AIPCon 10. Construction is getting a second flagship.
  • February 2026: DevCon2 introduced Embedded Ontologies for offline edge devices, explicitly citing mining and construction sites with unreliable networks, a direct fit for off-highway equipment telemetry.

The integration

IrisX as a certified telematics source for Foundry construction ontologies, following the Cavanagh pattern where dispatch, trucking and site operations run on equipment, GPS and truck-scale data. Ontology platforms synchronize rather than sample, so one Foundry deployment pulling continuously across a whole fleet is plausibly a high-throughput, durable IrisX credit consumer (assumption). IrisX surfaces: the Time Series API feeds hours, fuel, location and fault streams into Foundry machine objects; GraphQL serves the asset and fleet structure those objects hang on; the MCP Server becomes relevant later for AIP agents querying live fleet state.

First 30 days

  • Ask Trackunit sales and customer success for any customer or prospect that has run or booked a Palantir bootcamp, or that mentions Foundry in RFPs. One named joint prospect is the go/no-go trigger.
  • Build one demonstration ontology object, a machine object fed by IrisX Time Series and GraphQL data, reusable for other AI platform conversations regardless of the Palantir outcome.
  • Watch Cavtera: as a construction-focused Foundry implementer it needs telematics feeds for every future customer, and it may be a faster door than Palantir itself (inference).
  • Open the EMEA business development conversation (London, then Copenhagen) only after the demo asset exists, so the first meeting shows data in an ontology rather than a slide.

Main risk

Deal count. Palantir deployments are few, large and slow, construction may stay a niche vertical, and there is no partner program to institutionalize the relationship. Mitigation: cap effort until the joint prospect exists, keep the demo asset platform-neutral, and treat Cavtera and McCarthy-style contractors as alternate routes to the same consumption.
