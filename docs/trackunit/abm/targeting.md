# Targeting: from 37 to five

ABM lives or dies on account selection. Everything downstream, the briefs, the plays, the outreach, inherits the quality of the list, and a partner program owns too few hours to spend them on accounts that will never consume. My root metric is IrisX credit consumption, so effort concentrates where consumption potential is highest, not where the logo is biggest. And the selection has to be auditable. If a colleague disagrees with an account on the list, they should be able to point at a score and argue with a number, not with my taste. That is also why the funnel below is documented end to end: any account that gets ABM treatment can be traced back through a disposition, a six-dimension score, and a sourcing category, and any account that does not gets a written reason.

## The funnel as it ran

This is the Source and Qualify discipline from the [method](../method/index.md), applied to one platform in July 2026.

**Step 1: 37 candidates.** The [landscape](../partner-mapping/landscape.html) maps 37 integration partner candidates across six categories: ERP (6), rental management (10), fleet software (5), ConTech (7), AI platforms (5), and OEM ecosystems (4). Sourced from public material: press releases, integration pages, marketplace listings, trade coverage.

**Step 2: scored on six dimensions.** Every candidate got a 1 to 5 score on Strategic Fit, Market Reach, Technical Readiness, Commercial Fit, Consumption Potential, and Recruitability, with the total as the simple average. Consumption Potential is the dimension that carries the root metric: expected credit pull, judged on frequency, breadth of assets, and depth of data. Recruitability matters more than usual right now because the field consolidated hard in 2025 and 2026, and several otherwise attractive accounts are already spoken for.

**Step 3: dispositions.** Scores convert to a disposition: 8 Pursue, 13 Deepen, 14 Monitor, 2 Deprioritize. Pursue means no integration exists and recruitment starts now. Deepen means a partnership already exists and the work is expansion and instrumentation. Monitor means real potential blocked by timing or M&A, revisit on a trigger.

**Step 4: five plays.** From the top of the scored list, five plays got full dossiers, the [First Five](../partner-mapping/first-five.md). Not the top five rows mechanically: the five were chosen so that each one anchors a repeatable play type and together they cover the whole map.

## What the funnel filters out

The bottom of the table is as informative as the top, because it shows the scoring is doing work.

**Document Crunch (1.8, Deprioritize).** Trimble agreed to acquire it in April 2026. The account routes into the Trimble ecosystem and away from neutral partners, so its Recruitability score is 1 and no hours go there.

**Genie / Terex (2.0, Deprioritize).** Genie Lift Connect was built with ZTR Control Systems. Interaction stays standards-based via ISO 15143-3 only; there is no bilateral partnership to build.

**Tenna (2.7, Monitor).** A structurally good fit as a mixed-fleet AEMP consumer, but John Deere's acquisition closed in February 2026. Neutrality and recruitability are gone, so it sits on Monitor with an M&A trigger rather than absorbing outreach effort.

## Tiering and cadence

**Tier 1 is the [First Five](../partner-mapping/first-five.md):** full account plans exist for [Procore](../partner-mapping/accounts/procore.html), [BearingPoint ETM.next](../partner-mapping/accounts/bearingpoint-etm-next.html), [Wynne RentalResult](../partner-mapping/accounts/wynne-rentalresult.html), [Point of Rental](../partner-mapping/accounts/point-of-rental.html), [Microsoft](../partner-mapping/accounts/microsoft.html), and [Palantir](../partner-mapping/accounts/palantir.html). These get named plays, first-30-days actions, and a written kill risk each.

**Tier 2 is the next band of scores:** Hilti ON!Track, the frontier AI assistants, Autodesk Construction Cloud, inspHire, STAEDEAN, and Fleetio. No full account plans yet. They are tracked through the [signal radar](signal-radar.html): funding, M&A, product launches, and marketplace changes that would promote or demote them.

**Everything below is Monitor or Deprioritize,** watched for the specific trigger written into its landscape note, such as the Thoma Bravo and Nemetschek combination that currently freezes HCSS.

The whole matrix is re-scored quarterly. Tiering is a snapshot, not a verdict: Tenna scored higher before the Deere acquisition closed, and the next quarter will move rows again.
