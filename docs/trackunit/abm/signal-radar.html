How to Read This

From signal to play

  1. Every signal maps to a play. A trigger type selects the entry from the play library: a partnership announcement fires the ecosystem-entry play, an M&A or product launch fires the data-gap play, hiring and financial signals reshape who to call and when.
  2. Signals are reviewed on a fixed cadence. The weekly signal review in the ABM operating system decides which triggers convert into touches that week and which accounts get upgraded, like Palantir's monitor-until-trigger disposition.
  3. No signal, no touch. A signal is a reason to reach out this month with a message the account already cares about; density above shows where the why-now case is strongest right now.
