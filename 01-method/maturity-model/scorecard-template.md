# Partner Program Maturity Scorecard

Working diagnostic tool. Score each stage by verifying capability checkpoints (binary: present/absent) and measuring KPI performance against thresholds.

Scoring rule: a stage earns a maturity level when ALL checkpoints for that level and all levels below are present, AND KPI thresholds for that level are met. See [00-maturity-framework.md](00-maturity-framework.md) for full scoring methodology.

**Calibration sources:** KPI thresholds are calibrated from three layers. First, third-party benchmarks: Forrester (454-company partnership maturity study), McKinsey (ecosystem strategy research), Omdia (AWS partner ecosystem multiplier study), BCG (industrial aftermarket services benchmark), and HubSpot/Continu (state of partner operations data). Second, the 8-book evidence base documented in the evidence library (P-01 through P-50, E-01 through E-12). Third, operational experience from pan-European B2B partner program deployment. Where thresholds lack a published benchmark, the source column notes the calibration logic. All thresholds are adjustable per company context during the diagnostic process.

**Evidence reference coverage:** [P-NN] principle references appear across all stage documents. [E-NN] evidence entry references currently appear in Stage 5 (Deliver) and this scorecard only, as the E-01 through E-12 entries were created during the Stage 5 deep research session. Stages 1-4, 6, and 7 use [P-NN] principles and third-party benchmark citations. Additional [E-NN] entries (E-13+) will be created when the research agent executes Mission 1 per stage. 17 of 50 principles (P-02, P-07, P-08, P-17, P-18, P-24 through P-30, P-37 through P-39, P-45, P-50) are cataloged in the evidence library but not yet cited inline in stage documents. These principles map to the Onboard, Enable, and Co-sell stages and will be incorporated during the revision pass or when those stages receive research agent enhancement.

## How to use this scorecard

**For self-assessment (internal use):**

1. Print or copy the checkpoint tables for each stage.
2. For each checkpoint, mark present or absent. No partial credit. If the answer is "partially" or "in progress," the checkpoint is absent.
3. Determine the highest level where ALL checkpoints at that level and below are present.
4. Measure KPI actuals against thresholds for that level.
5. If KPIs fall short, score drops to the level below. Tag the gap as "infrastructure ahead of performance" in the gap report.
6. Plot the seven scores on the spider chart: 1 = Basic, 2 = Professional, 3 = World-class.
7. Identify the top 3 gaps by revenue impact.
8. Select 3-5 interventions from the stage documents' intervention libraries.
9. Execute in a 90-day sprint. Re-score. Adjust.

**For target company diagnosis (HVO production):**

1. Complete the intake questionnaire for the target company.
2. Score the company using public information, research agent output, and informed hypotheses where data is unavailable. Mark confidence level (high/medium/low) per stage.
3. Produce the spider chart overlay: target company's current state vs world-class.
4. Identify the top 3 gaps and estimate business impact per gap.
5. Write the gap into the HVO as the diagnostic section. The gaps create deal debt: the recipient sees a specific problem they had not articulated, scored against a benchmark they cannot dispute.

**Re-scoring cadence:** quarterly for active transformation programs. Annually for steady-state monitoring. Every re-score should show movement in at least one stage. Stagnation across two consecutive quarters signals a governance problem, not an execution problem.
