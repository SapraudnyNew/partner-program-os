## Part B: Evidence Entries

Sources cited inline in method and artifact files. Each carries a strength label:
- **Hard:** Independent research, peer-reviewed, or audited data.
- **Practitioner:** Domain expert citing their own data and experience.
- **Inferred:** Logical extension from sources; not directly cited.

### E-01: Bob Moore, *Ecosystem-Led Growth* (Wiley, 2024)

Founder/CEO of Crossbeam. Primary source for ELG concepts: Account Mapping Matrix, "pierce the veil," EQL, partner-sourced/influenced/joint, prisoner's dilemma of data sharing.
**Strength:** Practitioner.
**Used in:** Stages 4 (Co-sell), 5 (Deliver), all strategy layer.

### E-02: David Yovanno, *The Partnership Economy* (Wiley, 2022)

CEO of impact.com. Positions partnerships as third revenue channel alongside sales and marketing. Reports M1 Finance achieving 50% lower cost-per-quality-user via partnerships vs paid search.
**Strength:** Practitioner.
**Used in:** Strategy layer, marketing stages.

### E-03: Cisco Partner Field Guide and Commerce Workflow (CCW)

Cisco's published documentation on deal registration, order processing, partner tier definitions, and AR discipline. Industry-standard reference for tech channel programs.
**Strength:** Hard (vendor-published reference architecture).
**Used in:** Stage 5 (Deliver).

### E-04: dormakaba supply chain digitalization (SAP Business Network)

dormakaba's SAP Business Network implementation: single platform for proposals, contracts, orders, invoices, payments. Source for the "zero-paper" principle and AR enforcement automation.
**Strength:** Hard (vendor case study with measurable outcomes).
**Used in:** Stage 5 (Deliver).

### E-05: Atluri & Dietz, *The Ecosystem Economy* (Wiley, 2022)

Two McKinsey senior partners. Defines orchestrator vs participant, ecosystem necessities analysis, build/acquire/partner grid, "80% of failed ecosystems are governance failures."
**Strength:** Hard (synthesis from McKinsey client base of 450+ companies).
**Used in:** Strategy layer.

### E-06: Bamford, Gomes-Casseres, Robinson, *Mastering Alliance Strategy* (Jossey-Bass, 2003)

Foundational text on alliance design, governance, constellation strategy, and alliance capability. Source for "strategy before structure," "managing without control," constellation thinking, joint decision making.
**Strength:** Hard (academic + practitioner synthesis).
**Used in:** Strategy layer, governance.

### E-07: Atlas Copco dealer operations model

Atlas Copco's B2B dealer operations: 48-hour dispatch for stock items, FleetLink for lifecycle tracking, warranty management portal, four-tier support levels, EXW/FOB destination by tier.
**Strength:** Practitioner (industry-standard reference).
**Used in:** Stage 5 (Deliver).

### E-08: Hilti PMO model

Hilti's Project Management Office discipline: pre-order technical sign-off, site readiness gates, project lifecycle from design through commissioning, mandatory installation mobilization gates.
**Strength:** Practitioner (Hilti-published).
**Used in:** Stage 5 (Deliver).

### E-09: Webex Partner RACI Matrix (Cisco)

Cisco's Webex partner onboarding and customer onboarding RACI definitions. Reference structure for stage-by-stage RACI tables.
**Strength:** Practitioner (vendor reference).
**Used in:** All stages (RACI structures).

### E-10: Microsoft Channel Partner Program

Microsoft's tiered partner program: Co-op funds, MDF, marketing concierge for partners. Reference for marketing WITH/THROUGH/FOR partners.
**Strength:** Practitioner (vendor reference).
**Used in:** Marketing stages.

### E-11: Daikin install base monetization

Daikin's HVAC install-base practice: every commissioned unit registered with serial, end-customer contact, warranty terms; downstream service revenue grows install base value year over year.
**Strength:** Practitioner.
**Used in:** Stage 5 (Deliver), Stage 6 (Renew).

### E-12: Moore & Thomas, *Marketing Multiplied* (2018)

Channel marketing 4-pillar framework: TO / WITH / THROUGH / FOR. The "random acts of marketing" antipattern. Modern buyer's journey alignment for indirect channels.
**Strength:** Practitioner (Averetek-published).
**Used in:** Marketing stages.
