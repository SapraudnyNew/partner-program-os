## Quick start by maturity level

**If the company is at Basic across most stages:**

Run these seven plays (one per stage) to reach Professional foundation in 90 days:

1. Recruit: Ideal Partner Profile build (2-4 weeks, $)
2. Onboard: 30-60-90 joint action plan (2-3 weeks, $)
3. Enable: four-motion marketing audit (2-3 weeks, $)
4. Co-sell: deal registration overhaul (4-6 weeks, $$)
5. Deliver: spec sign-off gate (4-8 weeks, $$)
6. Renew: install base cleanup (4-6 weeks, $$)
7. Expand: portfolio coverage mapping (2-3 weeks, $)

Total estimated investment: 90 days, primarily staff time. No technology purchases required except deal registration (PRM or CRM workflow).

**If the company is at Professional and aiming for World-class:**

Run these downstream-first (revenue impact sequence):

1. Renew: connected fleet rollout (6-12 months, $$$) — the foundation for digital services
2. Deliver: install base capture at handover (4-12 weeks, $$) — feeds everything downstream
3. Co-sell: ABSM sprint launch (8-12 weeks per sprint, $$$) — highest pipeline ROI
4. Enable: self-service enablement platform (12+ weeks, $$$) — scales without headcount
5. Recruit: scoring matrix and 9-box (4-6 weeks, $$) — improves recruitment quality

Total estimated investment: 12-18 months, significant technology and process investment. Board-level budget approval likely required for connected fleet and enablement platform.
