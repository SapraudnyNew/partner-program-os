# Procore Technologies (NYSE: PCOR) - Web Research Digest

Researched: 2026-07-03. Focus: App Marketplace, partner ecosystem, Equipment Telematics, AI agents, EMEA. Facts only unless marked as inference.

## Snapshot

- ConTech platform company: project management, financials, quality and safety, resource management for construction. Founded 2002 by Tooey Courtemanche (CEO). HQ: Carpinteria, California (6309 Carpinteria Ave).
- Public: NYSE PCOR since 2021. FY2025 revenue about $1.3B; Q4 2025 revenue $349M (+15.6% YoY).
- Q1 2026 (reported May 5, 2026): revenue $359.3M, +16% YoY, non-GAAP operating margin 17%. FY2026 guidance raised to $1.499B-$1.53B.
- Headcount roughly 4,200+ (corporate fact sheet); PitchBook lists 4,421.
- International is about 15% of revenue; company targets 25% by 2027 (analyst commentary, treat as directional).

## Why now signals

- AI agents strategy is the center of the roadmap. Groundbreak 2024 launched Procore AI (Agents, Insights, Copilot). Groundbreak 2025 launched Procore Helix (embedded AI intelligence layer) and Agent Builder in open beta: customers build custom agents from natural language prompts; attendees built 1,000+ agents at the event.
- Datagrid acquisition (completed January 20, 2026, terms undisclosed): agentic AI platform that connects data across ERPs, document repositories and project platforms and orchestrates multi-step workflows (submittal reviews, RFI drafting, cross-system search). Datagrid CEO Thiago da Costa now leads AI and data strategy at Procore. Products offered to Procore and non-Procore customers.
- United Rentals strategic partnership (announced February 26, 2026): first telematics integration; United Rentals rental equipment data and telematics sync directly into Procore Resource Management, with AI-driven recommendations for resource deployment. Framed around closing the "resource gap" between office planning and jobsite execution.
- Tiered Technology Partner Program announced June 16, 2025 (blog: "Building Stronger, Together"): rewards partners on joint customer and business value, globally consistent structure, clear growth pathways. Owned under Ryan Butler, SVP Corporate Strategy and Operations. Tier names and criteria are not spelled out in the public post.
- EMEA expansion: new EMEA hub in Dublin announced, hundreds of hires planned (sales, CS, support). UK Data Zone live; EU Data Zone planned for fall 2026. Procore CDE (common data environment) launching first in UK and Ireland, then wider EMEA.
- Verdantix frames 2026 as a construction software consolidation year (Procore/Datagrid vs Hexagon and others).

## Hiring signals

- Careers page (careers.procore.com, ~109 open roles, checked July 2026): no roles with "partner/partnerships/marketplace/ecosystem" in the title currently visible in the first pages of search. Heavy engineering emphasis: Staff Software Engineer - Agentic Foundation (Cairo), Staff Software Engineer Full Stack (Datagrid) (Bay Area/Austin), Staff Integration & Application Developer (Austin), Software Engineering Manager AI Evaluation Platform (Austin).
- Third-party job boards (Zippia and others) list a Director, Partnerships, North America role ($188.7K-$259.5K base plus commission, reporting to GM North America) plus Partnership Manager and Partner Marketing Manager listings; freshness uncertain, may be 2025 postings. Inference: partnerships hiring exists but the loud investment is in agentic AI and integration engineering, which matches the Datagrid and Helix strategy.
- Cairo engineering hub hiring for "Agentic Foundation" signals durable AI platform buildout, not a one-off feature.

## Integration-relevant facts

- App Marketplace: launched February 2016 with 20 apps; now roughly 539 listed apps (appmarketplace.com count; Procore itself says "hundreds"). Public developer platform at developers.procore.com with open REST API, partner overview docs, and the Technology Partner Program.
- Equipment Telematics feature (in the Equipment tool, integrated with Resource Planning/Tracking): open API ingesting telemetry from Caterpillar, John Deere, Samsara, and now United Rentals (support docs list all four as configurable connections). Procore states "more integrations planned." Trackunit is NOT currently listed as an Equipment Telematics connection and no Trackunit app is confirmed on the Procore Marketplace from this research. Inference: this is the concrete gap a Trackunit/IrisX partnership would fill; Trackunit already appears alongside Procore in Hilti ON!Track's integration list, so co-ecosystem presence exists.
- United Rentals integration proves Procore will take third-party fleet/rental telematics natively into Resource Management, including AI recommendations built on that data. Inference: an IrisX connector (OEM-agnostic, mixed-fleet data) fits the same pattern and could feed both Equipment Telematics and Helix/agent use cases, driving IrisX credit consumption per API call/data stream.
- Datagrid is explicitly "data agnostic" and works across third-party systems; Procore's stated goal is eliminating data silos across ERP, documents and field systems. Inference: equipment/telematics data is an obvious next silo, and partner data feeds become agent fuel.
- Partner motion is formalized: tiered Technology Partner Program (June 2025), partner directory (partners.procore.com), Groundbreak partner experience track. Groundbreak remains the annual flagship event for ecosystem announcements.
