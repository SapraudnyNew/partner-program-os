# Wynne Systems (RentalMan, RentalResult) - Web Digest

Researched: 2026-07-03. Facts sourced from public web; inferences marked.

## Snapshot

- Wynne Systems, Inc., HQ Irvine, California. Part of Volaris Group, an operating group of Constellation Software (TSX: CSU); Volaris acquired Wynne from United Rentals in May 2012.
- Two flagship products: RentalMan (enterprise rental ERP) and RentalResult (construction equipment and tool management, acquired with Result Group in December 2015). Portfolio also includes Logistics Solution, Service Solution, MobileLink, RapidCount, RenterLink e-commerce.
- Size: roughly 100-120 employees (PitchBook 116, RocketReach 117; LinkedIn 51-200). Revenue not public; third-party estimates range from $6.5M (Latka, likely stale) to $16.4M (RocketReach 2025). Treat as low-tens-of-millions, Constellation-style profitable niche software (inference).
- Leadership: Clare McCormick promoted to General Manager; Robert Gray moved to Chief Revenue Officer focused on new markets and closer customer partnerships. Product managers: Jim Rosinke (RentalMan line), Craig Richmond (Result line).
- Structure note: sister company InTempo Software (also Volaris) serves local and regional rental operators; Wynne focuses on national and multinational enterprise accounts. This split dates to the 2015 reorganization, not a recent move (an undated Wynne page resurfaces it; treat "December 2025 reorg" claims in aggregators with caution).
- Key customers: Wynne states RentalMan powers the top four global rental companies, including United Rentals, Sunbelt Rentals (Ashtead), Herc Rentals, and Loxam. So yes, United Rentals runs RentalMan and is also Wynne's former owner. Turner Construction's First Equipment Company runs RentalResult.

## Why now signals

- SAP ETM sunset campaign: SAP ETM usage rights in S/4HANA compatibility mode ended in 2025 and maintenance ends 2027. Wynne runs a dedicated landing page positioning RentalResult as the purpose-built replacement, with content refreshed as recently as February 2026. This is an active migration window for contractor equipment fleets.
- RentalResult appears to be getting its own brand surface: wynnesystems.com/sap-etm-sunset now 301-redirects to rentalresult.com. Inference: Wynne is spinning RentalResult out as a distinct contractor-facing brand.
- January 2025: Wynne named Foresight Intelligence (Fleet Intelligence) its "preferred telematics provider" for RentalMan. Not stated as exclusive, but it is a competitive wedge against Trackunit inside Wynne's telematics stack.
- Samsara integration announced for dispatching and scheduling with real-time location in RentalMan workflows.
- Product cadence: Q3 2025 RentalMan release (IntelliSource, Logistics, Service, RapidCount, MobileLink updates); a Logistics Solution coming to RentalResult "in spring" (2026); Re-Rentals Direct now auto-creates RentalMan pickups from RentalResult requisitions, connecting contractors directly to rental suppliers.
- Event: Wynne User Summit 2026, October 5-7, Charlotte, NC. Concrete co-marketing and field-engagement opportunity.

## Hiring signals

- Wynne's Workday board (volarisgroup.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/WynneNA) shows only 3 open roles as of July 3, 2026: Enterprise Account Manager (UK, virtual), Director of Sales (US, TX/remote), Senior HR Business Partner (US). All posted 30+ days ago.
- No open partnerships, platform, integrations, or AI roles. Inference: Wynne grows through sales into existing enterprise accounts and the SAP ETM displacement motion; integrations are handled by the existing product org (Rosinke/Richmond), so a Trackunit partnership conversation lands with product management and the CRO, not a partnerships team.
- The UK Enterprise AM plus EMEA history (Wynne Systems EMEA, RentalResult's UK roots) signals continued European enterprise push (inference from role location).

## Integration-relevant facts

- Existing Trackunit integration: Wynne maintains an official Trackunit integration page covering RentalMan and TMS (transportation management), with real-time location, utilization, and maintenance data flows. A corresponding "ERP Connector - RentalMan" listing exists on the Trackunit Marketplace under the @trackunit namespace (listing page is app-rendered; publisher appears to be Trackunit itself).
- Risk/nuance: the Foresight Intelligence "preferred telematics provider" designation (Jan 2025) means Trackunit is one option, not the default, in RentalMan telematics. An IrisX-native, deeper connector (off-rent triggers, utilization billing, service workflows) is the counter-play (inference).
- Wynne advertises integrations of RentalMan and RentalResult with dozens of third-party systems: ERPs, CRMs, telematics, e-commerce. RentalResult specifically integrates with SAP, CMiC, eCMS, JD Edwards, Sage, Salesforce, Oracle, and Vista Viewpoint.
- Trackunit angle for the role: (1) upgrade the legacy RentalMan connector into an IrisX Marketplace app consuming IrisX credits across United Rentals/Sunbelt/Herc/Loxam-scale fleets; (2) ride the SAP ETM migration cohort by pairing RentalResult (system of record for contractor equipment) with IrisX as the equipment data layer; (3) Wynne User Summit (Oct 2026) as an activation milestone. All three are inferences on top of the facts above.
