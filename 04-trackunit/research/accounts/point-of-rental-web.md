# Point of Rental Software - Web Digest

Prepared 2026-07-03. Facts from public sources; inferences marked as such.

## Snapshot

- Rental and inventory management software vendor (ERP for rental businesses). Product family: Expert and Elite (US flagship products), Essentials (entry level), Syrinx / Syrinx 365 (UK-origin product), Point of Rental One app, and the new Rental Intelligence Suite.
- HQ: Fort Worth, Texas (14401 Statler Blvd), with offices in the UK (Berkshire), Australia (Melbourne), South Africa (Johannesburg) and Canada; roughly 305 employees across 5 continents (RocketReach, June 2026).
- Customer base: 5,000+ business locations in about 80 countries, across equipment, event, tool, portable sanitation and specialty rental verticals.
- Ownership: private, backed by Susquehanna Growth Equity (SGE lists Point of Rental as a portfolio company). Note: the "part of Kojo" hypothesis in the brief is not supported by any source found; the Kojo entities in PitchBook/Crunchbase are unrelated companies. Revenue not public; 2025 was its 10th consecutive year on the Inc. 5000 fastest-growing list.
- Leadership: CEO Wayne Harris; first CFO Matthew Ross (Aug 2024); first CTO Emad Georgy (Jan 2025, ex-Windows OS and IBM Rational contributor, founder of AI agency NeoPath); Collin Pike, VP Cloud and Innovation, fronts the AI suite.

## Why now signals

- Jan 2025: long-term partnership with Trackunit announced (see below). Framed by CEO Wayne Harris as "taking IoT to the next level of innovation with Trackunit."
- Mar 1, 2026: acquired The Owl, a rental data insights platform; PitchBook logs it as Point of Rental's most recent deal. The Owl's analytics are folded into the new Rental Intelligence Suite.
- Mar 2, 2026 (ARA Show, Orlando): launched Rental Intelligence Suite, described as an AI decision engine and "the first agentic intelligence layer built specifically for rental operations": natural-language MyDashboard, Analytics (ROI, utilization, repair cost), cross-fleet Benchmarks, nightly AI Insights, Disposition Planner, Daily Briefing, and background AI Agents. Trained on de-identified data from thousands of rental companies.
- Mar 2, 2026: also launched an AI-powered Intelligent Phone Agent for missed-call handling.
- Telematics partnerships beyond Trackunit: Hapn (GPS/telematics, "preferred partner," planned API-driven real-time monitoring and geofencing for Elite customers) and Matrix iQ (fleet intelligence, asset tracking, theft recovery for Syrinx 365 customers in the UK).
- Hiring of a first-ever CTO with an AI background (Jan 2025) plus a first CFO. Inference: PE-backed professionalization of the executive layer and a deliberate pivot from record-keeping software to an AI/data platform, which raises the value of machine-data feeds like Trackunit's.

## Hiring signals

- Careers page (Jobvite) as of July 2026 lists only ~5 open roles: Customer Success Manager (US), DevOps Engineer (US and Berkshire UK), Sales Development Rep (Fort Worth), Tier 2 Software Support (UK). Glassdoor shows 7 US openings.
- No open partnerships, integrations, platform, product or AI roles were visible. Inference: partnership and integration work is handled by the existing product/engineering org (CTO Georgy, VP Collin Pike), so a Trackunit partnerships motion should target those leaders directly rather than a partner team; also suggests the AI push is being built with current staff plus the acquired The Owl team.

## Integration-relevant facts

- Trackunit partnership (announced Jan 2025): two-way (bidirectional) API connection between Point of Rental Expert and Elite and Trackunit Manager plus the Trackunit Go app. Expert and Elite became the second and third Point of Rental products to integrate with Trackunit, after the UK Syrinx product (that link dates back to a 2019-era partnership).
- Data flows: on-rent/off-rent status, delivery, call-off and ready-for-delivery events pass between systems; combined with Trackunit's real-time machine visibility this feeds a customer portal for renters during the contract period; billing data is integrated to improve invoicing accuracy and cut manual entry. Quotes from Trackunit President Jeff Cohen ("connect the ecosystem even further and automate much more than ever") and CEO Wayne Harris.
- Trackunit Marketplace already lists an "ERP Connector - Point of Rental (Syrinx 365)" app, i.e. Point of Rental is a live marketplace/ecosystem partner, not just a press-release partner. Inference: every Syrinx 365/Expert/Elite seat that syncs rental status via these connectors drives Iris/IrisX API traffic, making Point of Rental one of the clearest credit-consumption channels among rental ERPs.
- Broader integration posture: a public partners-and-integrations directory; parallel telematics integrations with Hapn and Matrix iQ show Point of Rental treats telematics as a multi-vendor category. Inference: Trackunit is not exclusive here, so depth of the IrisX integration (billing, portal, AI-suite data feeds) is the competitive lever.
- The Rental Intelligence Suite consumes fleet and asset performance data (utilization, ROI, repair costs). Inference: telematics-grade machine data via IrisX would materially enrich these AI models, a concrete expansion path for the partnership.
