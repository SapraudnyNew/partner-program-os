# Trackunit: People and Likely Hiring Manager (Head of Partnerships - Integrations & Applications)

Research date: 2026-07-02. Sources: Trackunit leadership page, careers site (Teamtailor), LinkedIn job posting, The Org, press and trade articles, web search. Exa people search was unavailable (credit limit), so LinkedIn coverage relies on indexed search results.

## Key findings

- The role (careers.trackunit.com/jobs/7782454) reports to the "VP of Platform". **No person with the title "VP of Platform" at Trackunit could be identified in any public source** (leadership page, careers people directory, The Org, LinkedIn-indexed search, press). The title does not appear anywhere except in this job ad. Likely explanations: the VP of Platform is a very recent or not-yet-public appointment, an internal move not reflected on public profiles, or a role being hired in parallel (inference).
- The job sits in the "Go-To-Market EMEA" department on the careers site, was posted around late May / early June 2026 (the careers page shows no date; the LinkedIn listing was about one month old as of early July 2026), is hireable across Denmark, Norway, Germany, France, Sweden, UK and Benelux (LinkedIn lists Kolding, Denmark), and describes collaborators including a "Head of Partnerships" and a "Platform Partnerships Lead (embedded in Product)". Neither of those two people is publicly identifiable either.
- The most probable organizational home for a "VP of Platform" is under **Mikkel Dalgas, Chief Product and Technology Officer** (on the leadership page; earlier title CTO, at Trackunit since 2017, ex Schneider Electric). He personally posts about the Trackunit Marketplace and integrations on LinkedIn (inference on reporting line; his role and posts are sourced).
- Product-side VPs who plausibly sit closest to the platform/ecosystem agenda: **David Swan, SVP of Products** (fronts product roadmap webinars, discussed the Marketplace launch, has a bio URL under trackunit.com/leadership-and-board/david-swan) and **Fred Rio, Senior Vice President, Products and Pricing** (ex Caterpillar digital lead; pricing relevant to the credit-based consumption model).
- Current executive team per trackunit.com/leadership-and-board: Soeren Brogaard (CEO), Laerke Ullerup (CMO), Evan Barnes (COO), Michael Tritschler (CFO), Mikkel Dalgas (CPTO), Anders Thorbjoern Jensen (EVP of Engineering), Martin Darre (Chief People Officer), Morten Gram (GM EMEA). Third-party org charts (The Org, RivalSense) show older data (e.g., Flemming Karstens Soeborg as CPeopleO, Ullerup as Chief Product & Marketing Officer), so treat aggregator titles as stale.
