## The situation

**Gap 1 — Recruit:** Axians/Fernao, SVA Wiesbaden, ACP Gruppe, AVANTEC AG, and InfoGuard AG are not in the program. All five have direct Guardicore product-gap fit. None have Illumio embedded. Full profiles, named executives, and first-contact hooks are prepared for each. The two structurally locked Illumio partners (KAEMI Berlin, Navixia Lausanne) are correctly mapped as non-recruitable.

**Gap 2 — Enable:** The Senior Channel Marketing Manager DACH role does not exist today. Ideal Partner Profile, partner campaign calendar, MDF activation framework, and DACH-native co-marketing materials are all absent. These can be built in 30 days with the entanglement research and partner profiles already completed.

**Gap 3 — Co-sell:** Computacenter — the largest DACH cybersecurity SI — sits at Select tier despite Cisco Gold, PANW Diamond, and top-20 DACH account coverage. A structured tier upgrade to Premier with a named-account co-sell plan converts a symbolic partnership into a commercial one. More broadly: Akamai holds 6sense intent data showing which DACH companies are in-market for cybersecurity right now. That data does not currently reach partners. Building a routing layer — monthly "Hot 20 Accounts" per partner territory, account-level only, GDPR-compliant, paired with pre-built German-language account kits — activates all five Pursue partners simultaneously. Microsoft runs this model at scale (Partner Center Cloud Ascent). The delta for Akamai DACH is operational: the intent infrastructure and partner portal exist; the routing layer does not.

The value here comes from the first: every co-marketing decision is a margin decision, every partner recruitment choice is a channel economics choice, every activation play is measured against pipeline contribution.
