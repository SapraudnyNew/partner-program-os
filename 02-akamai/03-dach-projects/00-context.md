# DACH Projects

Two execution projects produced as supporting artifacts for the Akamai HVO bundle, per ADR-009.

## Project 1: ABM/TAS DACH Partner Project

Location: `abm-tas-partners/`

30 partner candidates scored, 10 longlisted with full Ideal Partner Profile,
9-box prioritization, and one-page profile per partner. Demonstrates how
Akamai's DACH partner pipeline would be built using Target Account Selection
discipline applied to partner recruitment.

Methodology source: Stage 1 (Recruit) of the Partner Growth Method,
ABM/TAS skill section.

## Project 2: ABSM DACH Sprint

Location: `absm-sprint/`

Full 32-artifact Account-Based Sales and Marketing sprint executed for
Germany Mittelstand Zero Trust. 3 target accounts deep + 1 publicly-named
showcase account. Demonstrates how NIS2 regulatory pressure converts to
German Mittelstand Zero Trust pipeline through structured ABSM executed
jointly with channel partners.

Methodology source: Stage 4 (Co-sell) ABSM skill section. Full 6-stage
pipeline: Context Architect, Target Account Selector, Deep Intel Profiler,
Strategy Master, Execution Arsenal, Infrastructure and Launch.
