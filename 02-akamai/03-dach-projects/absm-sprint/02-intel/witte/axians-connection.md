## Why Witte Is Especially Right for the Sprint

Five reasons:

### 1. The ISO/SAE 21434 certification signals exceptional buyer readiness (✅ unique)

Witte just achieved certification (April 2025) as one of the first three automotive suppliers globally. This tells us:
- They invest in cybersecurity as a strategic differentiator, not a compliance minimum
- They have a mature internal organization (Cybersecurity Competence Center)
- They have a known audit partner relationship (TÜV NORD)
- They have a compliance-fluent culture
- They've publicly committed to ongoing investment

**A Mittelstand company that just spent the effort to get ISO/SAE 21434 certified is, by definition, willing to invest in cybersecurity tooling.** This is the highest-quality "warm signal" in the sprint set.

### 2. The post-VAST integration creates immediate urgency (✅ recent)

The June 2023 VAST acquisition is still being integrated. Each integration phase is a natural buying moment for security tooling. **Year 3 post-acquisition (i.e., now, 2026)** is typically when the IT integration consolidation budget materializes.

### 3. Quintuple regulatory cascade compresses the timeline (✅ documented)

Five overlapping frameworks (NIS2, ISO/SAE 21434, TISAX, UNECE WP.29, CRA) demand network segmentation evidence between now and December 2027. There is no buying-deferral path — at least one framework's audit triggers action.

### 4. No incumbent visible — clean opportunity (✅ confirmed)

Status C is SAP-process. flexis is sequencing. Neither competes with Guardicore. Axians IT Security entering the conversation faces no displacement risk — only the standard greenfield evaluation.

### 5. Customer-cascade messaging is unusually credible at Witte (✅ unique)

Whereas other accounts can be told "your customers will eventually ask about supplier cyber," **Witte is already being asked**. Every OEM TISAX assessment, every UNECE WP.29 certification, every PCSP / OEM internal audit lands on Marko Schwarz's desk. Stage 4 messaging can name this directly: "*Your customers are already asking. The next audit cycle is when documented segmentation becomes non-negotiable.*"
