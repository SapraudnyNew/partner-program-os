# Akamai Leave-Behind Memo

> **Status:** Skeleton. To be drafted in a dedicated session after Akamai-specific research is complete (see `00-context.md`).

## Format

3–5 pages. PDF for handover. The referrer hands this to the hiring manager before the call.

## Skeleton

```
[Cover paragraph — one line value claim]
  Example: "How a P&L operator approaches channel marketing —
  notes prepared for [Hiring Manager], DACH Zero Trust channel."

[Executive Summary — half page]
  Three to five points. Standalone readable. Forwardable to VP.

[Section 1: What I see in Akamai's DACH channel today]
  Outside-in view. Specific. Not flattering, not aggressive.
  Two or three observations grounded in research.

[Section 2: Where the largest moves are]
  Three priorities. Mapped to 7-stage lifecycle but not labeled as such.
  Each priority gets one paragraph with concrete first action.

[Section 3: What I would own in the first 90 days]
  Specific deliverables, not abstract themes.
  Three to five items. Each measurable.

[Section 4: Why this fit]
  Brief. Two paragraphs maximum.
  Connects 20+ years P&L to channel marketing in DACH cybersecurity.
  Frames the level fit explicitly, in Alex's own voice.

[Closing — half page]
  No "I'd love to chat." Referrer handles that.
  Sign-off line: "Happy to take this further with [hiring manager] when timing works."
```

## Specialization rules

Pull from the universal template (`01-method/`) but:

- **Reframe the language** for cybersecurity, not B2B equipment. Zero Trust, secure access, partner-led security adoption.
- **Reframe the scale** for Akamai's channel reality. They have hundreds of DACH partners; the operations focus is not order-to-handover but campaign-to-pipeline.
- **Lead with marketing**, not operations. The job is channel marketing.
- **Cite from the evidence library** sparingly. One or two refs maximum in the main body. Appendix is light.
