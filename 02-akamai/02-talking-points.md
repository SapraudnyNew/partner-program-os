# Akamai — Talking Points for the Warm Intro Call

> **Status:** Skeleton. Drafted alongside the leave-behind memo.

## Purpose

The leave-behind memo is what the hiring manager reads *before* the call. This file is what Alex prepares *for* the call itself. Different rhythm. The memo argues. The call listens.

## Call shape (45–60 min)

| Time | Phase | Alex's posture |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Pleasantries, referrer mention | Warm. Brief. |
| 5–15 | Hiring manager talks about the role | Listening hard. Two clarifying questions max. |
| 15–35 | Substantive exchange about channel marketing in DACH | Now Alex earns rank. |
| 35–50 | Hiring manager's questions about fit | Direct answers. No hedging. |
| 50–55 | Alex's questions | Three prepared questions. |
| 55–60 | Next steps | Let the hiring manager propose. |

## Three prepared questions for the end of the call

(To be customized after research is complete.)

1. **A diagnostic question:** Something that shows Alex understands the operational reality, not just the strategy deck.
2. **A constraint question:** What's the one thing about this role that the job posting doesn't capture honestly?
3. **A team question:** Who is the person on this team Alex would most learn from, and what are they best at?

## Three things to *not* do

- Do not pitch the leave-behind memo. The hiring manager has already read it. Referencing it once is fine; pitching it is not.
- Do not signal upward ambition. The hiring manager doesn't want to hire someone who's eyeing their boss's job. If the VP reads the memo and wants a separate conversation, that's the system working. Alex doesn't create it.
- Do not over-prepare answers. The strongest moments in the call will be unscripted. Over-rehearsal kills them.

## Three things to do

- **Ask before answering.** If the hiring manager describes a challenge, ask one clarifying question before proposing a frame. Senior managers rarely do this. The behavior signals seniority.
- **Be specific about money.** When channel marketing comes up, reference EUR. Pipeline targets, MDF efficiency, cost-per-quality-pipeline. Most channel marketers talk in activities and MQLs. Alex talks in P&L language.
- **Close with the referrer's name.** Final line: "Glad [Referrer] connected us." This is a small act of credit-sharing that the referrer will hear about.

## Post-call action (within 24 hours)

Send a five-line follow-up to the hiring manager:
- Line 1: Specific reference to something the hiring manager said.
- Line 2: One concrete thought Alex had after the call.
- Line 3: Resource or article that connects to their conversation.
- Line 4: Open invitation to continue.
- Line 5: Sign-off.

CC the referrer if (and only if) the hiring manager's tone in the call was warm.
