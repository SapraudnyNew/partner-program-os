## Top 3 gaps with revenue impact

### How to read the revenue numbers below

These are **order-of-magnitude estimates only.** Each rests on three layers of assumption:

1. **Market size assumptions** from outside-in sources (Mordor Intelligence, IDC, Grand View Research). Public TAM/SAM data, not Akamai's internal targets.
2. **Capture-rate assumptions** based on Akamai's public market position vs competitor share. Not verified against Akamai's internal share targets, which are gated.
3. **Velocity assumptions** based on "what does a 6-quarter delay in DACH channel marketing function cost in a market with NIS2 deadline pressure." This is the softest layer - no public benchmark exists for the specific velocity haircut.

Treat the dollar figures as directional, not committed. The qualitative argument (gap exists, it costs money, here is the rough scale) is the load-bearing claim. The specific numbers are illustrative.

**During the hiring interview, the candidate's job is to ask the hiring manager for the actual internal targets, then re-anchor these estimates on real data.** That conversation itself is part of the value the role expects.

### Gap 1: Enable — missing DACH channel marketing function (the role itself)

**What is missing:** No DACH channel marketing function exists. Marketing motions THROUGH partners (manufacturer content distributed via partner brand to partner audience) and FOR partners (manufacturer-funded marketing for small partners) cannot execute. No public DE-language NIS2 partner playbook surfaced in research. Campaign Builder localization for DACH status unknown.

**Why it matters now:** NIS2 affects 29,000+ German organizations [SOURCE: BSI 2024]. Window for partner-led capture of this mandatory-budget wave is 18-24 months. Competitors with staffed DACH channel marketing execute while Akamai builds.

**Revenue impact — directional estimate:**
- German Zero Trust market (outside-in): $2.47B (2024) → $5.6B (2030) [Mordor Intelligence, company file 02]
- Hypothesis: Akamai targets 5% capture by 2030 = $280M run-rate (assumption; needs validation against Akamai's actual DACH revenue target)
- Velocity drag from missing DACH channel marketing function: 6-quarter delay in operationalizing Motions 3 and 4
- Conservative impact estimate: 10-15% capture-rate haircut over 4 years = $28-42M opportunity cost
- Aggressive impact estimate: 20-25% haircut if competitors lock down NIS2 mindshare = $56-70M

**Caveat:** the velocity-drag assumption is the weakest link. The number should be read as "tens of millions over four years, exact magnitude depends on capture targets and competitive intensity, both of which require internal data to anchor."

**90-day intervention (D2-2 → D3-1/D3-2 feed):**
1. Stand up DACH content stack: 3 NIS2-Mittelstand-Zero-Trust assets in German (white paper, ROI calculator, partner-brandable webinar template) by Day 60
2. Launch motion 4 (FOR partners): manufacturer-executed campaign for top 3 anchor partners (DT Security, Bechtle, Controlware), partner brand on output, leads routed to partner by Day 90
3. Establish MDF cadence with ROI gates at quarterly cycle starting Q3 2026

### Gap 2: Recruit — KAEMI, Navixia, Mittelstand systemhauser not in program

> **Note: gap analysis below uses initial entanglement signals from existing research. Recruitability scores and final dispositions await D2-RC mission output (see ADR-011). What is presented here is the pre-Recruitability view; the gap remains but the specific partner-by-partner play may shift once entanglement layers are mapped systematically.**

**What is missing:** The Illumio EMEA Partner of the Year (KAEMI GmbH, Berlin) is in Akamai's home territory and is not in Akamai's program. Navixia (first EMEA Illumio ZTS Professional, Romandie) covers Switzerland's French region where Akamai has no partner. Mittelstand systemhauser (SVA, Cancom, Axians, Materna) sit outside the program despite being the channel to NIS2-affected Mittelstand. Computacenter (Cisco's most-decorated DE partner) is parked at Akamai's lowest tier despite publicly co-investing in Illumio.

**Why it matters now:** the Berlin-headquartered Illumio star not being on Akamai's roster is a daily competitive loss in the home market. Mittelstand Zero Trust capture without systemhaus distribution is structurally limited.

**Revenue impact — directional estimate:**
- Each strategic partner at Premier tier threshold: $2M+ annual revenue minimum (Akamai's published Premier criteria suggest this scale, exact threshold GATED)
- 10 net-new strategic partners at Premier = $20M+ annual revenue floor
- KAEMI/Navixia displacement is a multi-quarter play (entanglement layer 3 - both hold Illumio POY/flagship status); estimated impact per displaced partner if achievable: mid-single-digit millions per year in recurring cybersecurity revenue
- Computacenter Select → Premier upgrade (tier-upgrade play): minimum $1.7M annual revenue delta (Premier threshold $2M vs Select implied ~$300k); **but Recruitability score from D2-RC will determine if this play is viable or should be Contained**

**Caveat:** the displacement numbers assume zero entanglement friction. In practice, KAEMI's Illumio Partner of the Year status and Computacenter's Illumio co-investment likely make these Contain or Monitor dispositions, not Pursue. D2-RC will resolve. The Mittelstand systemhaus recruitment numbers (SVA, Cancom, Axians) are more reliable because those partners are not publicly entangled with Illumio at flagship level.

**90-day intervention:**
1. Build IPP with 6-dimension weighted scoring including Recruitability per ADR-011 (depends on D2-RC research)
2. Score 30 DACH candidates with disposition (Pursue/Contain/Monitor/Drop) - this is D3-1: ABM/TAS DACH Partner Project
3. Open recruitment outreach at top 3 Mittelstand systemhauser (high-Recruitability), open tier-upgrade conversation at Computacenter only if Recruitability score supports Pursue, treat KAEMI/Navixia per D2-RC disposition recommendation

### Gap 3: Co-sell — contested mindshare at distribution layer + Austrian greenfield

**What is missing:** Infinigate distributes Akamai, Illumio, AND Cloudflare MSSP (exclusive) in DACH. Arrow ECS CH holds both Guardicore (since 2017) and Illumio exclusive. Mindshare contested, no public Akamai Distributor Business Review cadence surfaced. Austria has no visible Akamai partner anchor.

**Why it matters now:** distributors carry 70% of new security business per Akamai's public statements. If Akamai mindshare at Infinigate sits at parity (33% across three vendors), correcting to majority (50-60%) via active mindshare management produces shift on the highest-volume channel.

**Revenue impact — directional estimate:**
- Infinigate DACH bookings split correction (33% → 50-55%) on Akamai security book: ~$5-10M annual swing toward Akamai
- Arrow ECS CH mindshare protection (Guardicore vs Illumio): preservation of estimated $2-3M Swiss Guardicore annual book against erosion
- Austria recruitment anchor pair (Cancom-KBC + ACP) ramp to $3-5M run-rate by Q4 2027

**Caveat:** these numbers assume distributor revenue concentration in DACH cybersecurity is in the high-single-digit-millions per distributor per year per vendor. This is plausible but unverified externally. Internal data would let the candidate replace assumption with actuals during 30-day onboarding.

**90-day intervention:**
1. Establish quarterly Distributor Business Review with named Akamai DACH executive ownership at Infinigate Deutschland and Arrow ECS Switzerland
2. Build contested-account playbook for distributor reps (when do you lead Akamai vs Illumio vs Cloudflare based on customer signal)
3. Austria gap-fill plan with Cancom-KBC + ACP as primary recruits
