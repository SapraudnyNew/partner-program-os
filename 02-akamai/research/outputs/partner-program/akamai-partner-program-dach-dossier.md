# Akamai Partner Program and DACH Partner Network Dossier
**Prepared for:** Incoming Senior Channel Marketing Manager, Akamai DACH
**Date:** 15 May 2026
**Classification:** Internal — Channel Strategy Working Document

## TL;DR

- Akamai Partner Connect (launched globally Q3 2025, announced via PR Newswire on 16 September 2025) is a unified program with EMEA-localized tiering across Select / Premier / Elite; for DACH, the practical roster is thin and top-heavy, anchored by Deutsche Telekom Security, Bechtle AG and Bechtle Schweiz, Controlware, InfoGuard (CH), and (at the lowest tier) Computacenter Germany — with distribution running through Infinigate and Arrow ECS.
- The Illumio DACH ecosystem — KAEMI GmbH (Berlin, Illumio EMEA Partner of the Year), Navixia SA (Ecublens, first EMEA partner to reach Illumio ZTS Professional level), Computacenter (a co-investor in both Akamai and Illumio), and Arrow ECS Switzerland (Illumio's exclusive Swiss distributor) — is the single most actionable displacement target list for Akamai Guardicore Segmentation in FY26.
- Recommended near-term investment posture: deepen Deutsche Telekom Security and Controlware as primary delivery anchors; upgrade Computacenter from Akamai Select to Premier; recruit three Mittelstand-facing systemhäuser (SVA, Cancom, Axians) onto Guardicore; and launch a structured displacement campaign at KAEMI and Navixia.

### 1.1 Tier architecture

**Akamai Partner Connect** was announced on 16 September 2025 (PR Newswire, "Channel Evolution: Akamai Consolidates Global Partner Program with Localized Tiering and Targeted Incentives") and rolled out globally in Q3 2025. It supersedes the legacy NetAlliance / Akamai Partner Program. The launch is the first time Akamai has integrated all partner types — resellers, distributors, service providers, TSDs, and referral partners — into a single global framework with regional tiering, per the public statement of Paul Joseph, Executive Vice President of Global Sales and Services at Akamai.

**Three tiers (Confirmed): Select / Premier / Elite**, per akamai.com/site/en/documents/brief/2023/akamai-partner-program.pdf and reaffirmed under Partner Connect blog content (akamai.com/blog/partners/introducing-akamai-partner-connect).

**Legacy global tier thresholds (Confirmed for 2023-era program; subject to EMEA localization under Partner Connect):**

| Tier | Annual Revenue | Net MRR | Min Certified Staff | Pricing Discount | Deal-Reg Discount |
|------|----------------|---------|---------------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Select | > $250k | > $7k | 3 | Up to 30% | Up to 15% |
| Premier | > $2M | > $50k | 6 | Up to 35% | Up to 15% |
| Elite | > $4M | > $100k | 9 | Up to 40% | Up to 15% |

Akamai's 2024 press coverage (ARN/IDG) cited an alternate set of legacy global revenue thresholds (Select >$100k, Premier >$2M, Elite >$4M) — the discrepancy with the brochure points to a tier-reset already in motion under Partner Connect.

🧠 **Inferred:** Under Partner Connect, EMEA-specific thresholds will be lower than global benchmarks. Akamai's own communication is explicit that "localized tiering reflects market maturity and rewards partner performance at the regional level." ❌ **Blind spot:** Specific EMEA/DACH revenue commitments per tier and the Annual Success Plan obligations are not publicly disclosed and must be obtained from EMEA channel operations.

**Tier benefits (Confirmed from 2023 brochure, carried forward):**
- *Sales*: Pricing discounts (up to 30/35/40%), additional ~15% deal-registration discount, new-customer rebate (max 15%), deal-registration protection at Select tier and above.
- *Marketing*: Akamai Campaign Builder access (all tiers); co-marketing, Partner Summit access, MDF and dedicated partner marketing support (Premier and Elite only); profile in Akamai Partner Directory; digital badges.
- *Technical*: Basic product training (all tiers); advanced product training and implementation training (Premier and Elite).
- *Relationship*: Partner Portal access, Akamai University access for all tiers.

### 1.2 Partner types and roles

Per Channel Futures (16 Sep 2025 coverage) and the Akamai partner blog, Partner Connect collapses all motions into a single program with **modular agreements**:
- **Resellers** (governed by the 2025 "Akamai Partner Reseller Program Guide", akamai.com/site/en/documents/corporate/2025/akamai-partner-reseller-program.pdf)
- **Distributors**
- **Service providers** (including Security Certified Service Providers — see Deutsche Telekom Security 21 January 2026 PR)
- **Technology Service Distributors (TSDs)**
- **Referral partners**
- **Global Systems Integrators (GSIs)**
- **ISVs** (being onboarded into the cloud ecosystem)
- **MSSPs** (explicitly named as the next motion to be expanded, per Joseph's statement to Channel Futures)

**Global SPIFF platform (Confirmed):** Rewards in 136 countries, 9 languages, 4 currencies. Eligible products are Akamai API Security and Akamai Guardicore Segmentation. Payouts are individual or company-wide, first-come, first-served (akamai.com/blog/partners/introducing-akamai-partner-connect).

### 1.3 Certification structure

Akamai University (akamai.com/global-services/services/akamai-university) issues credentials via the Credly Open Badge platform. Three role-based training tracks are referenced in Partner Connect content: **Account Executive**, **Solutions Engineer**, and **Solutions Architect**. Delivery modes: self-paced, live virtual, hands-on labs, and Akamai Test Account access.

**Recognized Guardicore-era certifications (Confirmed by name across Akamai materials and partner content):**
- **GCSE — Guardicore Certified Solutions Engineer**
- **GCSP — Guardicore Certified Service Provider**
- **APISP — Akamai API Security Professional**
- **AAP — Akamai Application Protector**
- **Akamai API Security Architect** (referenced under Partner Connect enablement)

❌ **Blind spot:** Specific prerequisites, exam structure, and recertification cadence per credential are gated inside partners.akamai.com and were not publicly retrievable. These must be sourced from the Akamai EMEA partner operations team.

### 1.4 MDF and co-marketing

**Akamai Campaign Builder** is the partner-facing campaign development tool. A May 2026 blog post by Prince Jason Albert (Akamai Global Channel Marketing Programs Manager) — "Your New Marketing Assistant: The AI Expert in Akamai Campaign Builder" — confirms an AI-augmented version is live (akamai.com/blog/partners).

🧠 **Inferred:** MDF allocation follows the Annual Success Plan/Business Plan negotiated with Premier and Elite partners; Select-tier partners receive Campaign Builder access but typically not direct MDF.
❌ **Blind spot:** Allowable activities, ROI reporting, disbursement cadence, and approval SLA are not publicly disclosed.

### 1.5 Deal registration

Confirmed: Deal-reg protection extends from Select through Elite (2023 brochure). A ~15% additional deal-registration pricing discount stacks on tier pricing discounts. ❌ **Blind spot:** Submission required fields, approval SLA, conflict-resolution policy, and Guardicore-specific protection windows are gated.
