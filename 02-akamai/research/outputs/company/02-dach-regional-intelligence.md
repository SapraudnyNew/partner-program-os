## Section 2.4: DACH Regulatory Exposure and Positioning

### NIS2 (Network and Information Security Directive 2)

NIS2, the updated EU cybersecurity directive, broadens the scope of regulated entities significantly. Germany's national transposition (NIS2UmsuCG) was due in October 2024 but implementation has been delayed. Akamai positions its security portfolio — particularly Guardicore Segmentation, API Security, and the broader Zero Trust platform — as directly supporting NIS2 compliance requirements. The German-language compliance page explicitly lists NIS2 alongside DORA and PCI DSS.

Akamai's DACH channel marketing materials reference NIS2 in the context of microsegmentation. Thomas Stark (Regional Sales Manager) posted LinkedIn content referencing API Security and NIS2 compliance together with hashtags #NIS2 and #Cybersecurity. This indicates NIS2 is an active sales narrative in the DACH field organization.

**Confirmed** for marketing positioning. ⚠️ No specific BSI certification or C5 attestation for Akamai was identified.

### DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act)

DORA, which applies to EU financial institutions from January 2025, requires operational resilience including ICT risk management, incident reporting, resilience testing, and third-party risk management. Akamai has produced extensive German-language content mapping the Guardicore Platform directly to DORA's five pillars:

1. **ICT Risk Management:** Microsegmentation reduces attack surface, AI-powered policy creation.
2. **Incident Management:** Guardicore's visibility enables faster detection and response.
3. **Resilience Testing:** Segmentation enables controlled testing environments.
4. **Third-Party Risk Management:** Guardicore can isolate third-party access.
5. **Information Sharing:** Threat intelligence capabilities.

The Commerzbank case study explicitly references regulatory pressure (including European Central Bank oversight) as a driver for API Security adoption. The Deutsche Telekom Security partnership press release specifically highlighted DORA compliance for financial services customers.

**Confirmed** for positioning. The DORA narrative is strongly aligned with Guardicore Segmentation and API Security — the exact products the Senior Channel Marketing Manager role will promote.

### BSI Certification / C5

❌ No BSI C5 (Cloud Computing Compliance Criteria Catalogue) certification for Akamai was identified. This is potentially significant for German public sector and critical infrastructure customers who require C5-attested cloud services. Akamai's cloud compute infrastructure (formerly Linode) may not yet have C5 certification, which would limit its competitiveness in certain German government and regulated industry segments. This warrants direct inquiry.
