## Section 4.1: Who Currently Leads DACH Channel Marketing

### Current DACH Channel Marketing Leadership

❌ **No current DACH-specific channel marketing manager or director was identified in this research.** This is the most significant finding of this section: the Senior Channel Marketing Manager role being posted appears to be a net-new position or a backfill for an unfilled vacancy.

The distinction between channel marketing and channel sales is important:

- **Channel Sales (DACH):** Led by **Robert Specht** (Director, Channel Sales DACH). He is responsible for partner relationships, partner development, and channel revenue in DACH.
- **Channel Marketing (DACH/EMEA):** The Senior Channel Marketing Manager role being posted would be the primary marketing counterpart to the channel sales organization for the DACH region, with an EMEA-wide scope for campaigns.

**Nadine Anders** (former Head of Channel Sales DACH, departed Feb 2025) was a sales leader, not a marketing leader. However, her departure may have created a gap in channel program execution that is now being addressed through both sales and marketing hires.

### EMEA Channel Marketing Leadership

🧠 **Inference:** The job posting states the role reports to a manager who oversees EMEA partner/channel marketing. The Senior Field Marketing Manager posting (also recently posted for DACH/EMEA) includes the instruction to "collaborate with Channel Marketing leaders (primary marketing PCOs)" — suggesting there is an existing EMEA Channel Marketing leadership function. The identity of the EMEA Channel Marketing Director or VP was not surfaced in this research.

The job posting describes the candidate as responsible for "EMEA partner marketing initiatives centered on segmentation and Zero Trust security solutions" — indicating this role has a horizontal EMEA scope with a DACH regional home base.

### Partner Programs Leadership: Pablo Onnias

**Pablo Onnias** leads Akamai's global channel programs (Partner Connect). He authored the September 2025 blog post introducing the program. His LinkedIn bio describes him as "leading Akamai's global channel programs, with a focus on empowering partners and driving growth through scalable, strategic initiatives." He has 20+ years of experience in UX, sales, product management, operations, and partner programs. Onnias likely oversees the global program framework within which the DACH channel marketing role operates, but he is not the direct hiring manager for this marketing position.

⚠️ The direct hiring manager for the Senior Channel Marketing Manager, DACH role was not identified.

## Section 4.2: Channel Marketing Team Composition

### Estimated DACH Channel Marketing Headcount

Based on LinkedIn analysis, no currently active Akamai employees with "channel marketing" in their title and "DACH" or "Germany" in their location were identified. This suggests the DACH channel marketing function is either:

(a) Unstaffed and being built from this hire as the anchor, or
(b) Covered by a small EMEA-level team without DACH-dedicated staff, or
(c) Historically part of a combined channel sales/marketing function under Nadine Anders that was not replaced when she departed.

🧠 **Hypothesis:** The Senior Channel Marketing Manager, DACH is the first dedicated channel marketing hire for the DACH region as part of the Partner Connect re-launch. This aligns with the September 2025 program launch creating a need for regional marketing execution that didn't previously exist as a standalone function.

- Adjacent roles: digital marketing, content syndication, events
- ❌ Exact team size unknown.

## Section 4.3: Recent DACH Channel Marketing Activity

### Akamai Partner Connect Launch (September 2025)

The global Partner Connect program launched in September 2025. DACH-relevant features include:

- **Regional tiering:** Partners are evaluated against local performance benchmarks, not global ones. DACH-specific tier thresholds apply.
- **SPIFFs for Guardicore and API Security:** The global SPIFF platform specifically rewards partner sellers who drive Akamai API Security and Akamai Guardicore Segmentation opportunities. These are the exact products the channel marketing role is designed to promote.
- **Enablement tracks:** Program-specific enablement for API Security and Guardicore including incident response, compliance, and Day-2 operations.
- **Cloud partner programs:** Dedicated agreements for cloud services partners and volume-based discounts for cloud resellers.

### EMEA Channel Power Hour (March 2026)

Holger Kuntz promoted the "Akamai Channel Power Hour" for EMEA partners on March 4, 2026, at 14:00 CET. The session covered Akamai's 2026 strategic priorities, key security growth areas, top customer-impact use cases, and updates on incentives, enablement, and demand generation. This is an example of EMEA-level channel engagement that a DACH channel marketing manager would help execute.

### Deutsche Telekom Security Partnership (January 2026)

The January 2026 announcement of Deutsche Telekom Security as a Security Certified Service Provider is the most significant DACH channel marketing event in recent history. This partnership involves:

- Day-2 operations for API Security
- Managed services for Guardicore Segmentation
- Focus on financial services, insurance, critical infrastructure, and public sector
- Sovereign cloud deployment capability

The channel marketing role would be responsible for co-marketing activities with Deutsche Telekom Security, including joint campaigns, collateral, events, and pipeline-generation programs.

### DACH-Specific Campaigns

Thomas Stark and Jan Römer (DACH sales team) actively share Akamai content on LinkedIn in German, referencing:
- API Security solutions with NIS2 and DORA compliance messaging
- State of the Internet (SOTI) reports with DACH-relevant statistics
- DNS security and Guardicore Segmentation content

This suggests ground-level marketing execution is happening through sales amplification, but no evidence of dedicated channel marketing campaigns (partner webinars, MDF-funded events, co-branded content) specific to DACH was found. This reinforces the hypothesis that the channel marketing function is being built, not backfilled.

❌ No specific Guardicore campaigns, partner webinars, or MDF-funded activities targeted at DACH were identified.

**Posted:** ~May 13, 2026

**Title:** Senior Channel Marketing Manager (listed internally as "Channel Marketing Manager Senior")

**Responsibilities (extracted from posting):**
1. Developing and executing EMEA partner marketing initiatives centered on segmentation and Zero Trust security solutions
2. Driving collaborative go-to-market initiatives with strategic partners to generate opportunities and support regional revenue objectives
3. Aligning partner marketing efforts with regional sales priorities and segmentation growth goals
4. Collaborating with sales and regional marketing teams to implement partner-driven demand creation initiatives
5. Planning and executing integrated partner marketing campaigns across EMEA, incorporating executive events and partner-driven field activities
6. Overseeing campaign implementation across various EMEA regions and collaborative partner networks
