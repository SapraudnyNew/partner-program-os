## Section 1.1: Financial Profile

### Revenue Overview — Last Five Quarters

Akamai crossed the $1 billion quarterly revenue mark in Q1 2025 and has remained above that threshold in every subsequent quarter. The company reported FY2025 total revenue of $4.208 billion, a 5% increase year-over-year. In Q1 2026, revenue was $1.074 billion, up 6% YoY (4% constant currency).

Beginning in Q1 2026, Akamai restructured its revenue reporting into three solution categories: Security, Delivery and Other Cloud Applications, and Cloud Infrastructure Services (CIS). Prior to this change, the categories were Security, Delivery, and Cloud Computing. Prior-period figures have been recast to reflect this new segmentation.

### Revenue Segmentation Analysis

**Security ($590M in Q1 2026, ~55% of revenue):** The engine of the business. Security revenue grew 11% reported (9% constant currency) in Q1 2026, consistent with Q3 and Q4 2025 growth rates. In FY2025, security contributed $2.243 billion, up 10% YoY. Within security, the high-growth sub-segments are Guardicore Segmentation and API Security, which collectively generated $90 million in Q4 2025 alone (up 36% YoY). For all of FY2025, these two products combined for $293 million in revenue, up 43% YoY. The Q1 2026 earnings call confirmed that growth was "led by strong demand for our market-leading web application firewall, API security, and Guardicore segmentation solutions." The WAF product is the largest single security product by revenue but the growth rate is not separately disclosed. **Confirmed.**

**Delivery and Other Cloud Applications ($389M in Q1 2026, ~36% of revenue):** This segment continues its structural decline, down 7% reported YoY in Q1 2026 (-8% constant currency). CFO Ed McGowan attributed the steeper Q1 decline to the "wraparound impact of the Edgio transaction." In December 2024, Akamai completed the acquisition of select Edgio assets (customer contracts, patents) from Edgio's Chapter 11 bankruptcy for approximately $125 million, adding several hundred CDN customers and an expected $80–100 million in 2025 revenue. As Edgio contracts anniversary, the inorganic contribution creates a tougher comp base. Akamai projects mid-single-digit delivery declines for FY2026 with the rate moderating through the year. The four largest pre-pandemic CDN competitors (Lumen, StackPath, Edgio, and effectively Verizon Digital Media) have all exited or gone bankrupt, reducing competitive pressure. Pricing declines are moderating but remain negative. **Confirmed.**

**Cloud Infrastructure Services ($95M in Q1 2026, ~9% of revenue):** The fastest-growing segment. CIS revenue grew 40% YoY in Q1 2026 (39% constant currency), following 45% in Q4 2025, 39% in Q3, and 30% in Q2 2025. Akamai raised its FY2026 CIS growth outlook to "at least 50% year-over-year in constant currency" following the announcement of a landmark $1.8 billion, seven-year commitment from a "leading frontier model company" for CIS services, disclosed on the Q1 2026 earnings call. This is the largest customer deal in Akamai's history. It follows a separate $200 million CIS deal announced in February 2026 with "a major U.S. tech company." Revenue from the $1.8B deal is expected to begin ramping in Q4 2026 (approximately $20–25M in that quarter), with associated CapEx of $800–825M over the following 12 months (~$700M in H2 2026, the remainder in H1 2027). **Confirmed.**

## Section 1.2: Strategic Positioning

### The Three-Pillar Strategy

Akamai positions itself across three pillars: Security, Delivery (CDN), and Cloud Computing (Compute). The company describes itself as "the cybersecurity and cloud computing company that powers and protects business online." This framing, adopted fully by 2023, deliberately leads with security and compute over its legacy CDN identity.

**Security as the revenue engine:** Security accounts for 55% of total revenue and is the established profit center. CEO Tom Leighton's public statements consistently lead with security growth. The FY2025 annual results highlighted security as "$2.243 billion in revenue, up 10% YoY." Within security, the Zero Trust portfolio (Guardicore Segmentation, Enterprise Application Access/ZTNA, MFA, and the Akamai Guardicore Platform) and API Security (post-Noname integration) are the highest-growth products. The company's Application & API Protection (AAP) product — specifically App & API Protector (the WAF) — remains the largest single security product by revenue. It was named a Gartner Peer Insights Customers' Choice for Cloud WAAP for the fifth consecutive year in 2024 and named a Leader in the Forrester Wave for Microsegmentation in Q3 2024. **Confirmed.**

**Delivery as the cash cow in managed decline:** Delivery revenue declined 5% in FY2025 and is projected to decline mid-single digits in FY2026. Akamai is not investing heavily in new delivery features but is harvesting the installed base and using the CDN business as a customer acquisition wedge for security and compute upsell. The Edgio asset acquisition brought several hundred net new customer logos into the CDN funnel. Pricing pressure continues but is moderating as competitors have exited. **Confirmed.**

**Cloud Infrastructure Services (CIS) as the growth bet:** CIS is the company's biggest strategic pivot. Launched on the back of the 2022 Linode acquisition ($900M), the compute business has matured from a developer-focused IaaS offering into an enterprise-grade platform. The Q1 2026 milestone — a $1.8 billion, seven-year commitment from a frontier AI model company — transforms the CIS narrative from aspiration to validation. Leighton described it as proving that "Akamai has what it takes to compete for and win very large cloud deals." CIS revenue guidance was raised to 50%+ YoY growth for FY2026. The company expects "significant acceleration of our overall revenue growth heading into 2027 and beyond." **Confirmed.**

### The Compute Pivot: Milestones, Skepticism, and Capital Allocation

Akamai's compute story has evolved through several stages:

1. **Linode acquisition** (March 2022, ~$900M): Acquired a Linux VPS/IaaS provider with a loyal developer base but minimal enterprise penetration.
2. **Akamai Connected Cloud rebrand** (2023): Unified Linode infrastructure with Akamai's existing 4,400+ PoPs under a single brand.
3. **Akamai Inference Cloud launch** (Q3 2025): Partnered with NVIDIA to offer GPU-accelerated inference at the edge using NVIDIA Blackwell infrastructure. Positioned against centralized hyperscaler AI by emphasizing low-latency, distributed inference close to end users.
4. **Fermyon acquisition** (December 2025): Added WebAssembly-based serverless functions (FaaS) to complement EdgeWorkers and enable edge-native applications.
5. **$200M CIS deal** (February 2026): First mega-deal validating enterprise AI demand.
6. **$1.8B CIS deal** (May 2026): Landmark commitment from a frontier model company. The "largest customer deal in Akamai history."

Analyst skepticism has centered on: (a) whether Akamai can compete against AWS, Azure, and GCP on compute; (b) the margin compression from CapEx investment; and (c) whether the CIS revenue is sustainable or concentrated in a few large deals. The Q1 2026 call addressed this: Leighton stated "We have a large and rapidly expanding pipeline of prospects... including some with very large needs" and noted deals across multiple verticals and geographies. CFO McGowan confirmed the $200M deal was with an "existing customer" who "dramatically increased their spend" and was "100% additive" — not a trade-off against delivery spending. **Confirmed.**

### Divestitures and Business Line Shutdowns

No material divestitures have been announced. The company has not exited any geographic markets. However, the structural decline of the Delivery business is a de facto managed wind-down: Akamai is not investing in new CDN capabilities and is using the base as an upsell platform. Akamai has not publicly shut down any product lines, though the Neosec brand was effectively retired when Noname became the unified API security platform. **Confirmed.**

## Section 1.4: Recent Moves and Signals (Last 12 Months)

### Earnings Call Commentary Trends

Across Q2 2025 through Q1 2026, four consistent themes emerge:

1. **AI as tailwind:** Starting in Q2 2025, Leighton framed AI as "providing a strong tailwind for Akamai." By Q3 2025, the narrative escalated to "Akamai Inference Cloud, powered by NVIDIA AI infrastructure." By Q1 2026, the $1.8B deal was positioned as proof that "the AI opportunity is real and Akamai is winning." This is the dominant strategic narrative.

2. **Security growth consistency:** Security growth has been remarkably stable at 9–11% YoY (constant currency) for four consecutive quarters. Guardicore + API Security growing at 36–43%. This is the recurring reliability theme — no acceleration but no deceleration either.

3. **Delivery stabilization:** The CDN business went from double-digit declines (FY2024: -14% to -18% in individual quarters) to low-single-digit declines by Q4 2025 (-2%). The Edgio customer migration contributed, but improved traffic trends and reduced competitive pressure also helped. Management expects continued mid-single-digit declines in FY2026 but the narrative has shifted from "free fall" to "managed decline."

4. **Partner-first repositioning:** The September 2025 launch of Akamai Partner Connect signals a structural shift. The program includes regional tiering, SPIFFs for Guardicore and API Security specifically, and dedicated enablement tracks. This is directly relevant to the DACH channel marketing role.

### Product Launches (Last 12 Months)

- **Akamai Inference Cloud** (Q3 2025): GPU-accelerated AI inference at the edge using NVIDIA Blackwell infrastructure.
- **Fermyon Wasm Functions** (March 2025, pre-acquisition partnership): Serverless WebAssembly on Akamai Cloud.
- **Akamai Partner Connect** (September 2025): New global channel program replacing legacy partner tiers.
- **CIS revenue segmentation** (Q1 2026): Cloud Infrastructure Services reported as a separate revenue line for the first time, reflecting its strategic priority.
- **Akamai Guardicore Platform enhancements** (ongoing): Multi-Factor Segmentation, Essential Policies, Worksites delegation, AI-powered features for simplified user experience.
- **LayerX acquisition announcement** (May 14, 2026): Browser-based AI usage control and secure enterprise browser capability.

### Strategic Partnerships

- **NVIDIA:** Akamai Inference Cloud uses NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs. Leighton stated that "NVIDIA referenced Akamai as a vital player in the industry's ecosystem for AI infrastructure" at GTC.
- **Frontier model company (unnamed):** $1.8B, seven-year CIS deal.
- **Major U.S. tech company (unnamed):** $200M CIS deal (February 2026).
- ⚠️ Hyperscaler partnerships (AWS, Azure, GCP) and GSI alliances not specifically researched in this cycle. Akamai has existing marketplace presence on AWS and Azure.
