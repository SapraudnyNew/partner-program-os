## Section 5.1: Strategic Risks Specific to the Role

### Risk 1: Is the Partner-First Repositioning Stable or Equivocating?

The September 2025 launch of Akamai Partner Connect, combined with the dedicated SPIFF platform for Guardicore and API Security, the Deutsche Telekom Security partnership, and the hiring of dedicated channel marketing roles, signals a genuine structural shift toward channel-led growth. Pablo Onnias's blog post explicitly acknowledged the failure of the prior "one-size-fits-all model" — a public admission that the old approach was inadequate.

However, Akamai's DNA is historically direct-sales-led. Security revenue of $590M per quarter has been built largely through direct enterprise sales, not channel. The channel contribution percentage is not disclosed, and the fact that the DACH channel marketing function appears to be built from scratch (not backfilled) raises the question of whether channel investment has been consistently underfunded. The departure of Nadine Anders (Head of Channel Sales DACH, 6 years) in February 2025 — just five months after Partner Connect launched — is a yellow flag. If the company's most experienced DACH channel leader chose to leave shortly after a major channel program investment, this could indicate skepticism about execution, resource allocation, or organizational commitment to the channel model.

**Assessment:** The Partner-First shift has real structural investment behind it (Partner Connect, SPIFFs, hiring), but the execution risk in DACH is meaningful. A new hire should validate (a) whether the DACH channel budget is incremental or reallocated from existing marketing, (b) whether field sales leaders (Philipp Merth) are aligned with and incentivized on channel-sourced pipeline, and (c) why Nadine Anders left.

### Risk 2: Is Guardicore Growing as Expected or Under Competitive Pressure?

Guardicore Segmentation and API Security collectively generated $293 million in FY2025 revenue, up 43% YoY, and $90 million in Q4 2025 alone (up 36%). By any measure, this is strong growth. Akamai was named a Forrester Wave Leader for Microsegmentation in Q3 2024. The DORA compliance narrative provides a powerful regulatory tailwind in financial services.

However, the competitive landscape is intensifying. Illumio — the direct microsegmentation competitor — continues to invest in its own channel and enterprise programs. Zscaler's Zero Trust platform is expanding aggressively. Palo Alto Networks and CrowdStrike are both moving into segmentation-adjacent capabilities through their platforms. The fact that Guardicore's co-founder (Pavel Gurvich) departed in November 2024 to found a new cybersecurity startup (Tenzai, which has already raised $75M) raises the question of whether he sees the Guardicore product's trajectory differently than Akamai leadership does.

**Assessment:** Guardicore's financials are strong, but this role requires understanding whether DACH-specific competitive dynamics differ from global trends. German enterprises may have different vendor preferences, and the channel partner ecosystem for microsegmentation in DACH is not well-mapped. The candidate should probe the win/loss ratio for Guardicore in DACH specifically.

### Risk 3: Is the DACH Region a Growth or Maintenance Market for Akamai?

Evidence for growth: (a) Three German cloud compute regions (Frankfurt core, Hamburg distributed, Berlin distributed); (b) Deutsche Telekom Security partnership specifically for DACH; (c) Multiple open DACH roles (channel marketing, field marketing, security sales specialist); (d) Active DACH sales team winning awards.

Evidence for maintenance: (a) No DACH revenue disclosure (possibly because it's not large enough to be material); (b) Channel marketing function appears to be net-new rather than expanding; (c) Austrian and Swiss presence unclear; (d) Employee reviews suggest cost-cutting and limited travel budget.

**Assessment:** DACH appears to be in a transition from "maintenance" to "selective growth." The Partner Connect program, Deutsche Telekom partnership, and new hires suggest investment, but the total investment level is modest compared to what hyperscaler competitors spend in DACH. A new hire should validate whether the DACH growth mandate has executive sponsorship and budget protection.

### Risk 4: Is the Senior Manager Rank a Stepping Stone or a Ceiling?

The "Senior" designation in this role is ambiguous. At Akamai, the named executive officers hold EVP and SVP titles; the operating committee includes SVPs and VPs. Below that, Director and Senior Director titles appear in regional roles. A "Senior Manager" title in marketing is typically mid-level — above individual contributor but below Director.

Kununu and Blind reviews consistently flag limited career advancement in EMEA: "Career chances seem difficult at least in EMEA" and Career Growth rated 3.2/5.0. Akamai's organizational structure is Cambridge-centric, and EMEA marketing leadership positions above the regional manager level are likely limited.

**Assessment:** For a candidate seeking long-term career growth at Akamai, this role may be a ceiling unless the channel marketing function in EMEA expands significantly. For a candidate seeking a 2–3 year platform to build a track record in partner-led Zero Trust marketing, the role offers strong content (NIS2, DORA, Guardicore, API Security) and a greenfield opportunity. The candidate should ask about the career path and whether the role can evolve to a Director-level position within 18–24 months if targets are met.

### Risk 5: What Does the DACH Channel Marketing Team's Stability Look Like?

The team may not yet exist. No current DACH channel marketing employees were identified. The role appears to be either the first hire in a new function or a solo position embedded within a broader EMEA channel marketing team. This creates two sub-risks:

- **If first hire:** The candidate must build the function from scratch — define KPIs, establish partner relationships, create campaign frameworks, and demonstrate ROI — all while navigating a new company. This is high-risk but high-visibility.
- **If solo position:** The candidate operates without a team, managing EMEA-wide campaigns with limited support. This is common in tech companies of this size but can lead to burnout, especially given the "save, save, save" sentiment from DACH employees.

**Assessment:** High-risk, high-opportunity. The right candidate would view the greenfield nature as a feature, not a bug. But they should negotiate for clarity on budget, headcount plan, and success metrics before accepting.

### Risk 6: What Does Akamai's Commitment to Channel Investment Look Like Across 12–18 Months?

The Q1 2026 earnings call and subsequent guidance emphasized two investment priorities: (a) CIS infrastructure CapEx (~$800M for the $1.8B deal), and (b) cloud sales and support team buildout. Security channel marketing was not explicitly mentioned in any earnings call.

Non-GAAP operating margin guidance dropped from 30% (FY2025) to 25–26% (FY2026 guide), and the margin compression is explicitly attributed to CIS investment. This creates budget pressure across the company. The question is whether marketing budgets — particularly channel marketing budgets in EMEA — are protected during this investment phase.

**Assessment:** Moderate risk. The fact that the role is being posted now (May 2026) suggests budget has been approved. But the candidate should verify whether the marketing budget is committed for a full 12-month cycle or subject to mid-year reductions.

## Section 5.2: Open Questions for the Candidate

These questions are designed to signal deep preparation and challenge assumptions in the job description.

**1. "What is the current channel-sourced pipeline contribution for security products in DACH, and what is the FY2026 target for this role?"**
*Why:* This tests whether the company has quantified the channel opportunity or whether the role is being created on instinct. If they can't answer, the role may lack clear success criteria.

**2. "Nadine Anders held the Head of Channel Sales DACH role for six years before departing in February 2025. How did her departure affect the DACH channel go-to-market, and how does this role complement Robert Specht's current Director Channel Sales function?"**
*Why:* This references a specific person by name, signals awareness of the DACH channel team dynamics, and asks the critical question about organizational design.

**3. "The Deutsche Telekom Security partnership announced in January was clearly focused on Guardicore and API Security Day-2 services for financial institutions. What joint marketing activities have already been executed with DT Security in DACH, and what is this role's expected involvement in expanding that partnership?"**
*Why:* References a specific partnership by name and signals understanding of the DORA compliance narrative.

**4. "The Akamai Partner Connect program launched in September 2025 with regional tiering and product-specific SPIFFs. What has DACH partner enrollment looked like since launch, and what are the top 3 DACH distribution partners for Guardicore and API Security today?"**
*Why:* Tests whether Partner Connect has traction in DACH or is still aspirational.

**5. "Guardicore Segmentation and API Security combined for $293M in FY2025 revenue, up 43%. How much of that came through the channel vs. direct, and does the company expect channel mix to increase in FY2026?"**
*Why:* Directly probes the strategic question of channel investment vs. direct sales dominance.

**6. "The Senior Field Marketing Manager role is also open for DACH/EMEA. How do these two roles coordinate — does the channel marketing manager focus on through-partner pipeline while the field marketing manager focuses on new-customer acquisition, or is there overlap?"**
*Why:* Tests organizational design clarity and signals awareness that both roles are simultaneously open.

**7. "Akamai recognized $55M in restructuring charges in Q4 2025. How did that restructuring affect the DACH marketing and channel organization specifically?"**
*Why:* Tests whether the hiring manager will be transparent about headcount changes.

**8. "NIS2 implementation in Germany has been delayed. How does the DACH sales team position Guardicore to prospects who are waiting for final NIS2UmsuCG guidance before investing in compliance solutions?"**
*Why:* Shows understanding of the German regulatory calendar and its impact on sales cycles.

**9. "Akamai's Israel R&D hub now includes Guardicore, Noname, and soon LayerX. For the DACH channel marketing function, how does the product roadmap from Israel translate into regional marketing campaigns? Is there a formal process for EMEA marketing to influence product positioning for European regulatory requirements?"**
*Why:* Tests the product-marketing-to-field-marketing pipeline and signals awareness of the Israel acquisition strategy.

**10. "Kununu reviews from DACH employees mention limited training budgets and restricted travel. For a channel marketing role that requires partner face-time, executive events, and field activities across EMEA — what is the travel and events budget for this position?"**
*Why:* Directly addresses a known pain point from employee reviews and tests whether the company has allocated appropriate resources.
