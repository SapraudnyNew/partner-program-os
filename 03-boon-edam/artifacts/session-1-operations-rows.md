## Operations rows for PDP Summary spreadsheet

Format mirrors existing spreadsheet columns: Area / Partner Expected Performance / BEGE Support / Differentiation by Tier.

### Row: Order Placement and Technical Specification

- **Partner Expected Performance:** submit spec-complete PO within 3 business days of commercial sign-off; include product model, configuration code, site dimensions, and access control integration specs; achieve 90% PO accuracy rate (no revisions required)
- **BEGE Support:** deal registration confirmation within 24 hours; ERP product configurator access; technical pre-order review for specials and retrofits
- **Differentiation by Tier:** Silver: standard configurator access / Gold: dedicated technical pre-order review / Platinum: joint technical sign-off with BEGE engineer

### Row: Contract Administration

- **Partner Expected Performance:** execute end-customer project contract using BEGE-standard templates; contract must cover scope, payment milestones, lead times, site readiness obligations, and acceptance criteria; close contract within 10 days of PO
- **BEGE Support:** contract template library (standard and Global Account versions); legal review for GA projects; co-signing on Platinum GA contracts
- **Differentiation by Tier:** Silver: templates only / Gold: BEGE legal review / Platinum: BEGE co-signing on GA projects

### Row: Order Confirmation and Lead Time Management

- **Partner Expected Performance:** communicate confirmed delivery window to end-customer within 24 hours of receiving BEGE order acknowledgment; report any site readiness risks that may affect delivery window
- **BEGE Support:** 24-hour order confirmation SLA for standard products; 5 business days for specials and retrofits; tier-based delivery priority on constrained inventory
- **Differentiation by Tier:** Silver: standard queue / Gold: priority allocation / Platinum: first allocation; dedicated PM contact for lead time updates

### Row: Transport Coordination and Inbound Logistics

- **Partner Expected Performance:** confirm delivery address, site access conditions, and unloading equipment 14 days before confirmed delivery date; manage inbound freight from BEGE staging to job site; notify BEGE of any delivery change 5 days in advance
- **BEGE Support:** freight cost-sharing model by tier; outbound from BEGE warehouse within 48 hours for stock items; confirmed shipping documentation per order
- **Differentiation by Tier:** Silver: EXW (partner owns inbound freight) / Gold: freight cost contribution / Platinum: FOB destination, BEGE manages delivery to site

### Row: Site Storage and Pre-Installation Staging

- **Partner Expected Performance:** provide compliant storage conditions (BEGE storage guidelines: dry, flat, protected from impact); confirm storage area available 7 days before delivery; complete BEGE pre-installation storage checklist
- **BEGE Support:** pre-installation storage and handling guidelines; checklist template; site audit support for Gold and Platinum
- **Differentiation by Tier:** Silver: guidelines and checklist / Gold: remote storage audit / Platinum: optional BEGE site visit before delivery

### Row: Financial Operations and Payment Discipline

- **Partner Expected Performance:** execute payment milestones per APA; maintain zero AR overdue; submit invoices to end-customer within 5 days of milestone trigger; DSO target per tier
- **BEGE Support:** milestone-based invoice schedule in partner portal; working capital support discussed at APA level; digital invoice management
- **Differentiation by Tier:** Silver: 50/50 (order/pickup), DSO target 60 days / Gold: 20/30/50 (order/pickup/handover), DSO target 45 days / Platinum: 20/30/50 with extended terms, DSO target 30 days; AR overdue suspends new order processing for all tiers

### Row: Site Readiness Verification

- **Partner Expected Performance:** submit completed site readiness checklist to BEGE 5 business days before scheduled installation start; checklist covers structural openings, power supply, integration points, fire safety clearances; 95% on-time submission rate (Gold/Platinum)
- **BEGE Support:** site readiness checklist template; technical review of submitted checklist within 2 business days; GA project: BEGE PM co-signs readiness certificate
- **Differentiation by Tier:** Silver: self-certification / Gold: BEGE technical review of checklist / Platinum: BEGE PM co-sign; installation order not released without confirmed readiness

### Row: Project Milestone Tracking

- **Partner Expected Performance:** report project status at each milestone gate (order confirmed, delivery confirmed, storage ready, site ready, installation started, handover complete); use BEGE PM dashboard for Gold and Platinum; monthly status updates for Silver
- **BEGE Support:** PM dashboard access (Gold/Platinum); milestone reporting template (Silver); dedicated project manager for GA projects (all tiers)
- **Differentiation by Tier:** Silver: monthly email update / Gold: PM dashboard access + bi-weekly check-in / Platinum: dedicated BEGE PM, weekly status sync

### Row: Handover and Documentation Package

- **Partner Expected Performance:** deliver complete handover package to end-customer at project close: signed installation report, commissioning certificate, end-user training completion record, warranty registration confirmation; submit installation report copy to BEGE within 2 business days of handover; confirm product registration in BEGE system
- **BEGE Support:** handover checklist and document templates; NPS call initiated by BEGE within 7 days of confirmed handover; product registration portal access; 3-month follow-up call by BEGE for Gold and Platinum
- **Differentiation by Tier:** Silver: handover checklist mandatory / Gold: BEGE NPS call + 3-month follow-up / Platinum: BEGE NPS call + 3-month follow-up + project closure review with BEGE PM

## Known caveat (from Alex's review)

- The "Gold: PM dashboard access" item assumes partners have PM tooling capacity. In reality, most BEGE partners work manually and by phone. Production rollout needs to substitute lightweight tools (email digest, shared Excel) for partners who don't have PM systems.
- Storage SLA enforcement is contractually documented but rarely invoiced. The intervention is operational discipline, not policy change.
- AR overdue automatic block is the highest-leverage single change identified in Session 1.
