# ADR-003: HVO Structure — 7-Stage Partner Lifecycle

**Date:** 2026-05-14
**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

The HVO Universal Template uses a **7-stage partner lifecycle** as its primary backbone:

**Recruit → Onboard → Enable → Co-sell → Deliver → Renew → Expand**

## Context

Two alternatives were considered:

1. **4-pillar functional split** (Operations / Sales / Service / Marketing) — mirrors the existing Boon Edam PDP Summary structure.
2. **7-stage lifecycle** — mirrors the way partners actually move through a partner program over time.
3. **Hybrid** — 4-pillar as structure with lifecycle as a cross-cut.

The 4-pillar option organizes content by which team owns it. The 7-stage option organizes content by where the partner is in their journey.

The hybrid sounds attractive but produces matrix-shaped documents that read like compliance manuals.

## Resolution

7-stage lifecycle wins for three reasons:

1. **Reader-aligned.** CEO and Head of Channel both think in lifecycle terms ("how do we get more partners to sell?" → that's an Enable→Co-sell question). 4-pillar forces them to translate.
2. **Source-aligned.** Bob Moore's Ecosystem-Led Growth, the strongest single source, is structured by funnel stage (demand gen → sales → success → expansion). 7-stage maps cleanly.
3. **Action-aligned.** A rollout map needs sequencing. Lifecycle has it built in. 4-pillar requires inventing sequence on top.

Functional ownership (the 4-pillar logic) appears inside each stage as a sub-section (RACI), not as the top-level structure.

## The 7 stages

| # | Stage | Purpose | Primary owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruit | Find and qualify the right partner | Channel team + CMO |
| 2 | Onboard | Move signed partner to first transaction | Channel ops |
| 3 | Enable | Build partner's capacity to sell and deliver | Enablement + Marketing |
| 4 | Co-sell | Joint pipeline generation and deal pursuit | Sales + Channel |
| 5 | Deliver | Order-to-handover execution (current BEGE focus) | Operations + Service |
| 6 | Renew | Retain and grow recurring revenue | Customer Success + Service |
| 7 | Expand | Cross-sell, upsell, new geo/segment | Sales + Strategy |

## Consequences

- The existing Boon Edam "Operations" section becomes "Stage 5: Deliver" in the universal template.
- The 50 principles from the deep-research synthesis map across stages, not into a flat list. Mapping documented in `01-method/appendix/evidence-library.md`.
